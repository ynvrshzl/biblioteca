I am fascinated and perplexed by this anomaly; Categorizing, Organizing, Managing and Building information systems is somehow more cognitively taxing than learning or researching straightforward, albeit, non-linear complex subject material, i.e., Physics, Mathematics, etc.

There are several Phenomenological and Neurological factors at play here, but one of the most interesting resources is Eleanor Rosch's "Prototype Theory," Vannevar Bush's seminal essay on "As We May Think," Claude-Shannon's "Information-Theory," Norbert-Weiner's "Cybernetics," General Neuroscience, aswell as the "Gestalt Psychologists."

These fields are fascinating and absolutely crucial to understanding how the top-level symptom of "categorization" leads to cognitive fatigue; and how to implement ergonomic, for stable methods for working with deeply complex materials.

The phenomenlogical activity is often; "files, folders, tabs, become lost in hierachies, notes, and context-shifting." Yet when studying or diving deep into subject material, or being engaged with an activity, i.e., writing music, appears to exhibit this "Flow" or "Flux" of both postiive nervous-system state, and neurological throughput. That's the "goldilocks-zone," the "horizon's edge" between the chaos of raw information, the order of structured systems, and the dynamic equillibrium of stability.

Examining this further, what is really going on between the Phenomenological experience and the Clockwork of Information? I suspect these are the invariatns that appear across complex fields, from Science, to Office-work, Academia, and General-Information-Management; Processual Lifecycles (Input, Process, Output. How information is stored, abstracted and processed computationally, and more importantly; how it is retrieved, re-discovered, linked and implemented in human life), Categorization (hierarchies of groups of attributes and relational clusters of information), Classifiacation (abstract properties of information), Standardization (Templates, Schemas, Patterns, etc.), Reliability (longterm system scaling, an emergent, yet phenomenologically verifiable property.)

In some sense, we are mirroring the memory-data-processing structures of the human Neuronal-and-Nervous-System. The complex part is...

There is most defninitely a co-relation between time, focus, attention (and other finite human resources) context-shifting, objective, equillibria and stability, rate of information streaming, future-orientation, planning, structure, systems. At a base level, Categorization as a raw activity exhibits little to none of these properties. Most especially, categorization, organization and effective mgmt, are integral to the sustainability and navigation of complex information systems. From code, to literature, even to organizing one's life outside the computer; Categorization is a large portion of sustainable systems, and consequently, incredibly cognitively taxing.

These studies aims at a manifold series of unified ideas, branching from the Phenomenological and the Machine of Information; identify and define information structures (how information is structured, proto-perceptually), how we categorize, classify and simply the world (gestalt/prototype), how we navigate complex enviornments without overhwlem (nervous-system/neurological sciences)
