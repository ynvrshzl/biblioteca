title: grammatica
---

Theory, what if letters had semiotic, pictographic meaning? This was inspired by a railroad sign in China I saw, where the letters "x-ing" essentially read as "cross-ing". This made me realize; letters can be interpreted visually.

This means language can be used as a symbolic device, for complex visual problems, which are not usually benefited by lossy visual diagrams.

- Systems Design
- General Management
- Task management
- Code

Guide
"X" resembles a "cross" or "merge". The letter "V" resembles a decrease or filtering. While "W" resembles two "v". "Y" resembles ... "S" resembles a smooth interpolation between points, and "." resembles a point. "%" Resembles a division of points

These pictographs can be composed and combined to form higher-order signs of meaning, for more sophisticated problem solving.
