# "Ontological Domains": Of Spheres beyond space and time
A classified overview of our work, it's purpose in human development, and our age-old quest for answering the hardest questions.

Our work can be described as three major spheres of study: The metaphysical, The physical and the Experiential.

I have experienced all three, each at significant points throughout my life.

The significance of these three prototypes, is integral to understanding the nature of our work, and how each domain solves a specific sets of issues in nature. As of July 18, 2026, I hypothesis these are the three "Fundamental Ontologies", or "types of realities" as we may understand. Now, the significance of classifying different domains of "reality", is purely intellectual; until we understand how our work is a reflection of those fields. 

The observable is the physical. What is proven to be true, regardless of observation, bias or (...). This is where mathematics lives. This is where the brilliant (person) lives.
