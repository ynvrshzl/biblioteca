# Potentiality vs Kinetics
## 
Aggressive optimization

During my recent studies with Kybernetics... I realized a remarkable idea: you cannot agressively optimize work, tasks, projects or infiormation!

You can create templates, workflows, systems, and explain at the most granular of levels of details; but if is not used, it is simply stored potential.

This is especially prevalant in a polymath; we are constantly warping time-and-space in our own little neuroverse. If you agressively optimizie and constrain the polymath; they will feel suffocated by pressure. But if you allow them to freely explore, they will eventually shift into a state of being that resembles fluid dynamics; go with the flow, but shape the direction by Telos.
