# Nightmare sequence
Father angelo was terrified during his existential trip... "what if I die... if I come back... what if I am reborn as a lucky person this time? A beautiful woman? Whom gets everything as she pleases?" The monk laughs hysterically, coughing almost to death. "She is still not free from (disease?)" 

The monk chuckles "You believe death will free you from this curse?... (chuckles)... let me show you something."

He sees the family he o nce had... his wife, his children... they all died tragically in a fire. He... He can... see them... for years he blocked the memories... but now... he's had enough... he has confronted the very abyss itself.

"Death is merely postponing liberation..." the monk open his eyes "and liberation, my friend, is the only escape."

Angelo yells maniacally "How may I be liberated?" The monk smiles... as he appears to crumble into dust... Angelo panics "No. No. No! Tell me! Tell me how may I be liberated of this curse!" The monk crumbles into sand, as the shifting sands of time continue forth...

Father angelo realizes... as he walks through the endless gallery of doors, opening one after another, peeking into the windows of people's lives... "There is truly no end to this madness..."

He turns to the monk "..." The monk smiles "Have you finally made your decision?" Father angelo is maniacally confused "decision?! What do you mean have I made a deicision? I have no such thing! I have no free will in this world gone mad!" THe monk laughs hysterically again "Were you expecting I say 'have you realized there is no end?'" Father angelo is absolutely furious and confused

Almost in a desparate absurdist poise, he kneels down and yells "Punish me for my insignificance." The monk laughs "What are you doing, Angelo?"
