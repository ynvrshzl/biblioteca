Domains of Cybeneering & Information Strata

In Cyberneering, all Forms of Systems Engineering may be traced back to these fundamental Domains:

- Top-down (excavation)
- Bottom-up (foundational) 
- Orthogonal/emergent (sampling)

Phenomenologically (roughly speaking...): A Mathematicians expands their Vocabulary while building upwards, while a Scientist examines the inner workings of the Vocabulary, drilling downwards to the most empirical of invariants. However, both the Mathematician and Scientist use the Volatile Power of Analysis

This is related to 2 models: Meta,Macro,Micro,Iota and Essence,Function,Form,Affect


Information strata deals with being at the correct level of representation & abstraction (information resolution,density,fidelity)
