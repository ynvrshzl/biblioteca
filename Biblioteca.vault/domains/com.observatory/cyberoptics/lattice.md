Decoupling vs. Coupling

In [Cyberneering](domains/com.observatory/Cyberneering.md), the most fundamental [[Operation]] is "Division," or Coupling vs. Decoupling.

Fundamentally,  Decoupling creates Visibility, while increasing communication infrastructure.

The relationships of the system (data flow, dependency, etc.) are primarily where Complexity Emerges (Nise, ENtorpy, etc.)

Decoupling in systems allows for visibility and advanced opreations, but requires extra communiaction infrastructure reponsibility.
