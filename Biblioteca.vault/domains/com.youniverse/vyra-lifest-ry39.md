
When Vyra falls into Existential Despair, her vivid alcoholic visions and nightmares, dreams... increasingly point her to the "Mind of the Designer."

"To say it is too much weight to bear... would be comically reductionistic of me... I am a grain of sand in an endless desert..."

"In my dreams I feel the full-depth of my Emotions... they are so powerful...but in waking life I am tormented by the lightning of my mind...what am I to do? Escape my body? Liberation? Salvation?"

This is where her mind breaks down progressively worse, as she questions the nature of reality

"Am I simply going insane, or seeing the truth no waking eye can peer?"

She has the most pleasant vision of alternate realities (this saved her life)... "These... these are my hands... this is my ... this is my life... my body... my memories... my emotions...."

"What is real?"

After reading Philsophies (similar to Sollipsism and Quantum Reality), Vyra realizes her old life has evaporated. She loses most of her memories, so the past is essentially gone. Her future is identity


"Time has collapsed. This moment is all I have... but in this moment... I want nothing more than to escape... I want to die... I have completely lost my mind."

She eventually needs to confront the Abyss and discover her "non-ego" self, her life-task.

"These are the seeds of my life, the seeds of time... of Consciousness...the Fingerprint of the Designer."

"How may we explain these visions? Alternate Realities?"

Vyra eventually collapses and surrenders: "The Human mind was designed... I do not know by whom or what... but I know of something of far more importance; this mind has a boiling point... there are so many things we cannot understand... cannot comprehend..."

After studying Philosophy and History, Vyra realizes she is not alone. "This is a great spirit of suffering... as old as these very bones..."

"One cannot break the boundaries of the 'Simulation' or 'Illusion' or 'Dream'... for we have this unexplainable"

"Perhaps the purpose of Human life is not to break the Matrix... but to correct it's mistakes."

That is where Vyra's eyes open... and she has Visions the opposite of the : Existential, visions of the Mundane.

"A hidden Logic to the cosmos."

---

Vyra eventually realizes "Whether or not there is a Creator to the Cosmos, or we simply evolved in an infinite Vacuum, or we live in a Simulation, or we are ghosts, or dreaming... after reading all of this madness, her very Psyche was torn to pieces... her life task was realizing not, that she could put the pieces back together; but that she needs something outside of herself, the Logos; the evidence of something outside of the self. Therefore, our responsibility and duty, (which was inspired by Lawrence's life's work and influence on her life...)"
