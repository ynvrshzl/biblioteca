Lawrence exclaims: "A world without pain, without suffering.. yes... yes that would be the ideal... the utopian's final dream. However, to dream of such a world, is to escape our duty in this world, in this life... Justice must be served... and some tasks almost seem to be assigned by fate... destiny... specific to each one of us... that infact is what provides the troubled psyche with the respite to confront the brutalities of everyday life."

"Now... don't get me wrong; To dream of a better life is noble... but to dream of another world, is fatal. For one worsens the very suffering one attempts to escape, when one does not confront suffering..."

But sir... how can we possibly deal with the magnitude of the whole world? What man can lay the weight of the World on his shoulders? What of the Human Condition? Are we not made to break and remake ourselves?
