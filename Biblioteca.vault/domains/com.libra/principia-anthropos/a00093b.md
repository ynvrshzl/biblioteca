Human Phenomenological-Physiological Suffering is unjustifiably underexplored and unrecorded. Most of History records war and crime of such depravity, that to merely know about these events, is surely to permanantly and fundamentally traumatize and change a person and how they see the world, and other Human beings. 

However, personal Suffering, either by situation, circumstance or... can be abstracted as 4 fundamental Domains: Internal, External, Bridge and Meta. If the internal cannot change, and the external cannot change, and the bridge between (action) cannot forge change then something outside of space-time must change the future as we predict it. Internal Psychology, External Physical, Metaphysical Spiritual.
