What if there exists classes of suffering... and what if one case of suffering is due to one's iniquities? You aren't everything you could be, and you know it. What if not only you suffer for all your decisions or indecisions, but what if those connected to your life, and what if the world in some sense, is a little closer to hell becuase of your iniquity?

Now, of course, one cannot feasibly pressure the weight of the world like Sisyphus. We operate within a Continnum of extremes; Grace is the Miracle, Not present in the Gradient.
