"You may eventually find, beyond the Abyss, a bedrock of fire, where no darkness prevails. ."
The above story, is single-handedly born out of the most traumatizing... in my life.

Infact, it is the trauma one endured, that crafted and shaped the self.

Infact, the preceeding idea is one of existential-gratitude: "One may discover a well of life, so profound, it justifies the conditions along our path. For we know now where the ship is steering, we know now the Telos which guides the Kybernao, in even the most darkest of maelstroms. One may also discover, within this storm, a figure of life so profound, one feels an exitential obligation, duty or responsibility to serve it and guide others towards it."

That may be an effective "Summation" of our philosophical... throughout our collective, shared human history.

The most important thing I learned throughout my descent into torment, was something that directly saved my life. I believe this is the most important thing I have worth sharing, becuase it saved me from ending my life: "What if all my suffering would one day transform into a live worth having lived?" I am living this belief today. Wether one believes in a benevolent heavenly father, or one may believe 'we are just randomly here.'
