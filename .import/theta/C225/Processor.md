"Idea for a tracker-based workflow"

For calendar todo's such as "replace x every y days", it may be beneficial to record quantity of various items, habits and data, and let the engine itself process the data according to rules. 

e.g., on Tuesday, person "x" records their entire dataset. This dataset can be a spreadsheet, database, template or any general data model that allows for the recording of fields and keys (tracking data and statistics). This system simply records various fields to keep track of, such as "almond oil quantity" and "habit x". The purpsoe of this is to cognitive overhead of tracking countless information inputs within an information system, and... allow the engine/processor to automate. e.g., when "almond oil quantity is below 5%, auto-add to notifications: purchase new almond oil."

This system also attempts to solve the "blind-spot" phenomenon, by providing an interface for memory; the main template contains variables, fields and keys that are significant to the human being's life, but may not always be tracked daily. In this sense, the system fills the role of "augmentive, cybernetic interface, administrative, executive memory."

## Elements of this system
A single repeating spreadsheet/template. This in and of itself creates a habit (principle of muscle-memory).
