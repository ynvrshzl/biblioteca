---
title: "Constraint enables creativity"
---

This is a principle of sustainable human intellect and applicatory cognitive abilities.

In applied practice, I learned that when we have access to an entire language, I become overwhelmed by multiple sensory streams of ideas. Models, designs, systems, long-term implications, principles, etc. 

But when the grammar is just small enough to be useful: it can be composed, extended and built from needs, rather than from exploration. **This is a key idea.**

This is a landmark principle in alot of creative work. 

For our Software Engineering work, the mechanics of ["Constraint Sets"]() sources directly from ideas explored in these studies.
