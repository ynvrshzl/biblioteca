# Why is lack of knowledge, painful?

Why is it painful to not understand problems in life? "Why  does this keep  happening to me? Why do I keep repeating the same mistakes? It feels like i'm stuck in a cycle. Am I ever getting out of it?"

At this stage of the human experience, we must turn to an equally chaotic study enviornment; __The Mythos.__ 

## Idealism issue
This problem, i think, can fundammentally be resolved at the foundational structural layer of the problem; whic is our belief architecture.

Practically speaking, this is the problem of __idealism-__ we often want things to be "ideal." We want things to work out. According to our plans, efforts, intelligence, etc. But I think the basis should be, "life is fundamentally painful, unpredictable, and it's a miracle that we aren't in constant pain. Change is the only constant."
