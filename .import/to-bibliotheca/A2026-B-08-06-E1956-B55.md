# Priority Template
Template for prioritizing or categorizing work, decision, projects, etc.  So the problem with prioritization... perhaps this template solves the components of priority, rather than "this is just important." Or over-engineering "this is why this is important," rather "this is a general area of a larger goal (telos); it's on topic."

## Components
Components of priority

- Urgency
- Complexity estimation
- Expertise (if known)
