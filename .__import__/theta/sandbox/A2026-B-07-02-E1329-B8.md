# Why is agency single-threaded? 

## Origin
In computation terms, "thread" is synonymous with "channel" or "stream". In essence, a thread is passage through which things travel, **sim. a bridge for things.**

## Examples
Why are books arranged one page at a time? Why can we only explore one path of a forest at a time?

## SIgnificance
Why does it matter that we are only capable of doing one-thing-at-a-time?

This becomes a problem when **the rate of our ideas, or experience, or phenomena, exceeds our capabilities for processing, either into action or understanding.**

For example, those of us who have more ideas than we can ever keep up with, may feel like we have several lifetimes of work stacked against us. This is, the central focus of this study.
