# Life cannot sustainably be about achievement.
Feeling exhausted actually makes me angry becuase I'm too tired to fight, but I know if I had a little more health I could live so much more... 

For me, chronic fatigue is not "just feeling tired", it's war. Between the life I deserve, and the life my body can support. Sometimes, I push myself through exhaustion, knowing it isn't healthy, or optimal, but survivng another day. I believe it is difficult to distinguish between overwork and survival... but perhaps... only time tells
