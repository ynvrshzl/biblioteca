Eliza Beth's life story

She was severely traumatized, and almost died when she was very young.

Her youth was essentially stolen from her, and her dignity as a human being...

But she discovered a love for music... and then a little girl looked up to her... because she was so beautiful and so gifted... she could play the piano effortlessly... she could sing through her violin... she could write music as a second language... It is here when Eliza realized she had a second chance at life, in the little girl... "Maybe my life was a nightmare... but at the very least... in (the little girl) I can watch her dream."
