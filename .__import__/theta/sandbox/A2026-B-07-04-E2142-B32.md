Rest in Peace, John Hudson.

Date: 2026-07-04

Today, I found out that John Hudson (First seeon on the incredible YouTube Channel, SBSK)... Passed away 2 months ago. 

I woke up exhausted. From a terrible night of sleep and seemingly random panic. There was probably no muscle or bone in my body that could have convinced me getting up would be worth the struggle. Just as I was finishing up scrolling on my phone, I saw his video... I thought it would be another update on his research program... it genuienly saddened me that he had died 2 months ago... I thought someone would find a cure... that made me the saddest...

...and so I started feeling... like it was so unfair that this human being suffered so much in life... and in the end... never knew how much he changed people all around the world... I read severla comments from people that said his life saved them from commiting suicide. There are no words to express how important that is. Even if he did not "beat" epidermyolis bullosa (EB) in his lifetime, one thing is for sure; he did not lose to it.

I remembered him form time to time, which means he had a significant impact; enough that my noisy mind could still remember him, years after hearing his story. I remember how scary his condition looked. I couldn't imagine what my life would have been like if I had his condition, and that's exactly the purpose of his life story... even before his words, simply by existing, his attitude towards life was his choice...alot of people feel sad, angry, resentful...

There is a persona in the Youniverse, a scientist-gone-superhero who cures disease. In another life, John Hudson...

This scientist-superhero is part of the Lost Angelis...

To honor his life, John Hudson is most defenitely a leading figure in my Youniverse, and shared through our Youniversal ontology.
