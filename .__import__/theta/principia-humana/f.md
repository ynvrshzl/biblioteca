I am absolutely fascinated by polymaths of the ancient past... people who have influenced the world we live in today, positively. solving problems with their brilliance, applying their unique set of abilities, and not merely "pushing human development" but "solving the hardest problems, expanding our capabilities to solve problems, or simply bringing Joy to the world."

People like Isaac newton, Leonardo da vinci, and even non-polymaths like Ada lovelace, who balanced being a mother, and is one of the rare female figures in computer science history. 

and then the greeks, Aristotle, Plato, Socrates, and the Roman emperors, Marcus aurelius, the stoics, and people who were not necessarily ideal, but left recorded fragments of human knowledge, wisdom, phenomenology, for us to study from today. that's honestly one of the most fascinating aspects of history; it is independent of substrate, requiring only externalization of medium, as the device of transmission! that's a discussion fascinating enough for its own separate section.

then we have famous inventors, such as Nikola Tesla, and trailblazers of fields, such as statisticians who discovered cures to diseases, not through the primary field of medicine, but through the introspection of data analysis, sometimes even before modern computers existed. 

and then we have controversial figures, artists, such as Michael Jackson, who impacted people, not only with his work, but the immense amount of suffering he endured in his life. 

in some sense, I believe creating a compendium of human beings, People who proved with their lives, change is not only possible; it is just as real as the biggest problems we face uman beings. in some sense, the invariant principle of this work, is providing evidence for this belief; there is a solution to every problem in life, it is found in life not in death. 

in fact, that is a belief that is guiding essentially my life's philosophy "imagineering." what a human being is capable of within a lifetime, is still vastly unknown. what we are capable of when the imagination meets engineering, when potential needs actuation, when the hardest problems become visible, mapped, and solvable, we don't know the limits of that! and I believe a work to compile significant human history, figures, epochs, inventions, etc, a work like that is absolutely fascinating!
