## 1.0 - Abstract 
Who is this for? 

A person with adhd, high ambition, neurodivergence, polymath qualities, or simply a noisy brain with many ideas, may find it impossible to simply "prioritize" projects. This is, most likely, at to how we process multiple channels of abstract information simultaneously. whether it is the meaning of a project, the urgency, a deadline, or ambition, projects are not single dimension. There is almost no such thing as "what is the most important work? what's the best decision?" by simply considering priority alone. however, projects, decisions, work, does exhibit multi-factorial properties. these properties can be used to establish energy effort, aka priority.
