Context: Mary-anne-beth reminisces on her childhood, in a freightening, de-personalizing scene. She recalls when life was worth living, and she feels sick to her stomach. "This is what it feels like to be me? I feel... nothing. But that girl felt something... and I seemed to have forgotten it..."

This scene portrays one of the clearest images of Mary Beth's trauma. The depth of which her psyche is wounded is beyond direct description, that is why this scene is almost imagistic; It is meant to capture life through her eyes and through her experience, because description would only merely describe from an outside perspective.

Scene: two little girls playing in a green field, on a sunny day, the morning before their parents died.

A little girl squeals with joy: "Mary-anne! Mary-anne!" The girls play in a green field. Mary's thoughts echo into a faint glimpse of light... "Life was so beautiful back then... I miss those moments so much."
