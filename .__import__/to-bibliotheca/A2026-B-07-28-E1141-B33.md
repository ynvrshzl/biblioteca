### 1.0.3. Application
This equation is actually pretty useful becuase it helps guide the design of useful abstractions vs. complicated abstractions. That's synonymous with "Justified vs. Isolated complexity." This is one of the most important properties of systems design that I have observed, marks the difference between 
- A system that grows with you, and you actually use.
- A system that is too complex to justify.
