## 1.0 - Why do we think?

(Inspired by "as we may think" by Vannevar Bush).

### 1.0.1 - A brief research

Essentially this model begins with an interesting hypothesis; the reason we human beings think is not merely to imagine, self-sooth, or talk to oneself; we think becuase we have access to a non-arbitrary unique microcosm of access; which we use to carry action. In other words; people exhibiting any, or more of these traits (the inner creatives: HSP, sensitivity, neurodivergence, brilliance, and at the darker triad; psychopathy, narcissism, manipulation, etc.) are accessing, shaping, molding, and spending more time with this microcosm, than the person with average, low (or in some cases, null) self-introspection.

### 1.0.2 - Thought travels through time and space

In other words; when we think, we are warping through time and space, within our own little microcosm. Support of this, is human memories; recorded fragments of time and space, existing as recall; neurons can re-play those same neural patterns occuring when the experience was processed by the nervous system (and other complex systems) and stored in short-term neuronal memory (usually hippocampus)
