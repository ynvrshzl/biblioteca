composable literature 

i think the reason writing books is so challenging is for a multitude of reasons... but perhaps thinking of a book as if chapters are smaller books, each with their own little world, yet communication between parts so they are not isolated... is something very architecturally elegant and scalable!

i.e., instead of rebellion, we have "chaos series"

most of our ideas come to us as segments and fragments of scenes and ideas, rather than cohesive slices... it is the composition process compilation of continuity..
