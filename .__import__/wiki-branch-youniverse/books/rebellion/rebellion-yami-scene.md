The turnaround of Yami's life is when he decides "I am not afraid." He voluntarily faces the storm, and it completely collapses him... yet he pushes onward... in search of something... "I see... I see a light... It's... it's real... It is real... I want to save it..."

That light is a metaphor for the human will... the soul... to live. This is the source of life. This is the source of ethics, the source of virtue, that which keeps the engine of animal force from collapsing the being... we are continously becoming... shaping... metamorphosis... Yami sees it metaphorically, with his eyes closed... desperate not to reach it, but to hold it. Protect it. For something far greater than our senses can allow, is at work within us. For as Dawn said "We do not wrestle against this body, but against unseen forces."
