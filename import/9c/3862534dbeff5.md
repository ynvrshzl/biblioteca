The problem of non-linearity, or combinatoric explosion, is not fundamentally that there are "too many possible directions", or "information overload", and perhaps not even necessarily a loss of Tether or Telos, but fundamentally, two major issues at the structural-and-processing qualities of information:

1. the emergence of information properties: weight, value, function, relation, context role and responsibility
2. the missing scalar resolution (hierarchical arrangement of information density, in categorical, property form.)
