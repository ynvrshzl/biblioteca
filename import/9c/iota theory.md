# Iota Theory
A foundational theorem/theory observation in cyberoptics/information-studies.

We cannot make sense of the world (or any of it's spaces/dimensions) merely through the iota (the tiniest form of things), yet it is through the iota (the tiny building blocks and steps) that we synthesize (knowledge, action, theory, etc.) and progress in non-linear spaces.

How does this work? Let's examine the origin of this theory to first understand where it came from, how it works, and how to apply it to Cyberneering

## Origin 

This observation originates from __language;__ at the level of iota (individual characters (graphemes)).

We will almost surely, never edit nor read nor chunk words in a non-linear form. The iota travels (or is arranged to) afford linear transit in non-linear conceptual space.

Yet the conceptual information is of a higher order; scale; dimension, often traversing non-linear spaces, while arranged in a linear body.

This suggests the structure, the backbone is linear, while the processing or construction, is somewhwat of a 'torrent'; non-linear.

As soon as we have to re-arrange the letters of any given individual word, we often lose the ability to undersatnd the word itself, or even our position or locatlity of the individual iota, in relation to the word. 

(the next section attmempts to unravel this theory in it's hihger-scale propogation.)

This also applies to semantics; when we re-arrange individual words at sentence level, often, both the syntax (structure) and our orientation (processing) must change. Otherwise, the internal coherence of the passage, is damaged and as such, our processing (and orientation).

The entire syntactical structure, must re-adapt it's internal structure to avoid grammatical errors, while preserving the original semantic coherence of the subject of intention.[^1]

This also applies at the level of the paragraph; pragmatics.

This property of information and sensory processing, propogates across multiple scales. Perhaps, explaining how we are able to arrange non-linear spaces, in linear form, to actuate or understand the story of complexities.


Cyberoptics easily states that we must have the ability to move forwards and backwards (proceeding and preceeding) in multiple scales, spaces and dimensions. However, the iota is arranged linearly; the sequential linear arrangement of once non-linearity space, is known as "segmentation" which is a fundamental operation/description of information structures.


In Cyberoptics, the 'cursor' or Human Attentional Machinery, infact is non-linear in it's capabilities of directionality in spatial contexts. This is necessary for a linear passage of navigation in non-linear space.
