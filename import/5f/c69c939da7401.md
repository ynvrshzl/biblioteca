"Variable Attentional Balance"

[3]

"What if vision is both; Anatomical and Cognitive?" that is the beginning of Cyberoptics as a Metaphor. But this concept probes a unifying theorem; "What if our Attention (which is somewhat of a unification of anatomy and abstraction)... is split across 3 functions, each with their own problem to solve. 1, the function of attention, 2, the function of focus, 3, the function of periphery or environmental awareness."

Why does this matter? It is how we navigate the world... and that is almost Axiomatic; without navigation we don't survive very long.

Cyberoptic vision concept: 3 tertiary/split focus vision? This may be applicable to ADHD folks... but what if it is a natural artifact of our evolutionary Biology? One that can help us arrange stimulating, engaging cyber-enviornments?

This is not multi-tasking, so much as it is an avoidance of hyperfixation or understimulation

1. Primary (attention)
2. Secondary (focus)
3. Tertiary (periphery/enviornmental)
