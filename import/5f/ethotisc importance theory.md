How does importance emerge? By scale? By number of connections? This question originates from our work itself in-fact: "How did we discover our most important work?"

A Hierarchy of Value emerged, undoubtedly. Work had to be prioritized, but the problem of priority is not a problem of organization (otherwise, categories and classificatiosn would suffice; but they merely worsen the cognitive load.)

Especially when no items present distinguishable value or weight. Therefore, the system itself did not afford importance... so what is importance? Value? Weight? Worth? Do we invent value, or is value a constituent element of the Cosmos? If things are inherently valueable, important or worth an ideal... how does this influence and change our everyday lives?

Today, we are facing an information explosion. The good stuff is still in there, somewhere, laden among the excess waste... if one knows where to look, the path reveals itself... but today, before we can even decide where to look; our attention has already been robbed

The actual process involved distance from work: pondering, cogitating and reflecting on the work we have (several lifetimes of work)
