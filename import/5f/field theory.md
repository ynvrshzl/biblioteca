## Structural definition of Field Strategies
How do we actually study the Complex? Natural world & the Abstract:

Before we can make sense of a Field, there must be structure. Just as a forest is un-navigable without position, our Fields require navigability for Orientation. This inherits the study of Information & Sensory Processing[^1] Boundaries announced where things should be kept separate. Connections where boundaries should serve as doors or windows... and 


When instruments are organized... boundaries are separated... sharpened... announced... given a name... titled...

This is the beginning of the Field...

So why do we study Fields?

Categorizing of the World allows us to keep track of otherwise, undefined or expensive-to-process boundaries between things.

We categorize the world into subjects, into slices. Into parts, segments, chunks and observable cross-sections. This does not appear to reliably work for the study of Eventuality and Dynamics... Systems in real-time... Behavior... Complexity Emergence... Synergy... but it does work for the Discretely measurable.

We have two primary strategies for observing and studying the natural world and the Abstract; 1, Discrete-or-abstract representation and 2, Close-resolution reconstruction
