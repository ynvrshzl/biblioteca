Dawn eventually reveals why his heart was so heardened... 
"Perhaps it is the very pain I feel... that was a sign that I loved deeply... perhaps it is the very pain that is the only undeniable reality we know of. It is the reality of the pain... the undeniable love that I had for you... that has ripped me apart... time will heal these wounds... but this heart will always ache for you."

He tries in his most vuleranble moment to put into words, that the reality of pain is the only verifiable artifact of true love. That pain itself is necessary for the profound weight of love... just as responsibility is a response to the cruelty of pride. 

It is one of his most vulnerable moments of Dualism.
