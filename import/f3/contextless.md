What to do when you don't know what to do?

- Step 0: Distance...Perhaps you need distance away from the Sphere of Mind.
- Step 1: Panic. [^1]
- Step 2: Not everything needs to be Objectively useful to be Intrinsically Meaningful (Tactile Feedback Theory)
- Step 3:  Listen to body when Brain fog, Fatigue, etc.
- Step 4: Not everything needs to be Immediately meaningful to be useful in the future; Work compounds over time, and certain things are only visible in Eventuality (Not in Static space.)

Not every Objective needs to be Objective. Games are intrinsically meaningful. Are they useful? I don't know. Are they Objectively making the world a less insufferable place? Definitely depends on the Game, but I've seen in my life this is very true and possible.
