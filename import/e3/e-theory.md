# Title

## 1.0 - Abstract
Context & Purpose of this Work

These writings contain a chronological (somewhat coherent) unfolding of foundational theory, hopefully to help avoid information overload and serious cyber-sickness side-effects that we do not caution enough today.

This work harnesses the issues of information overload and the mis-use of technology, as an age-old problem, ancient even moreso than the history our ancestors obsessed over intellectualizing and preserving... These problems act as a sort of motivating force to plow through harsh problems and questions of Epistemology, Mortality, Phenomenology and What ought to be, in a world that is becoming increasingly obsessed with the Discrete and the Machine.

## 1: Definition
What is Education?

Education is something like the "proto-actuation code, strategy & communication of information travel, between people." It is how information travels history, memory and outlives even empires who birthed the minds who developed or observed such things. 

This is why this field/theory is concerned with the Humane faculty of Knowledge as a Relationship, not merely the Information mass or Theoretical material. The Education system is how we learn formally, and reliably (though it definitely needs infrastructural rework, a discussion for another time.)

## 2: Scope of this study
Analytics, Theory, Information and Abstract Reasoning. Education unifies these complex abilities of the Human intellect, as one coherent occupation and motivational structure. Which guides our behavior, thought, action (etc.) Education has profound implications on all aspects of Human Phenomenology.

The scope of this study, is a very particular, classical Academia broadcast strategy for Education, because I believe it is a format which is most inherit to how we Human beings actually understand the world, ourselves, and most importantly; an example of a medium acting as a buffer between what ought to be vs. what is (more on this later.)

## 3: Academic Excellence

Academic Excellence relies on Excellent Education. Garbage in, garbage out. Nourishment in, nourishment produced.

The Object of focus is The professor, The profession, The knowledge and the Medium: The chalkboard (or it's whiteboard derivatives. Importantly, we are not limited to analog, nor focused on the virtual. This study is focusing on the substrate-independent proper form of Education.

There are 3 particularly fascinating properties (read further for section) of this kind of Education. These properties exist somewhere in a Continuum of Extremes;

## 4: The extremes of Education

1. __External:__ Overly invested in engagement & participation e.g., open-mic, without emphasis on the technical or detailed foundations of the curriculum.
    
2. **Internal:** Lenient prescence in the process, e.g., textbooks, without the Humane approach or guidance.
    

In both cases however, invariant relata are always present; the Professor and the Profession. What seems to be missing, is the Medium, the Form; the Form of Communication. Naturally, we are looking for a medium that unifies both extremes. This is where the theory begins shaping into form:

## 5: Properties 
"3 properties of the particular class of Humane learning, which is quite important in Information Science & Education (And Incredibly important in today's hyper-information era)"

1. __Semiotics & Visual Dimension:__ General Visual information provides a buffer between verbose expression, and Human imagination. Doodles, arrows, sketches on board, they are not insignificant; presenting a tactile, tangible sensory source, while being tacit in nature. This means an image can compress "a thousand words" but in consequence, fail to specify a target of meaning: incomplete and implicit. A visual diorama lends next to nothing about it's context: which is where the next dimension naturally unfolds. It answers the problems at the Ontological and Phenomenological scales, e.g., "How do we apply this? How did we get here? What is this for? What is the purpose of this image?"

2. __Linguistic Dimension:__ Visuals rarely provide inherit affordances for meaning. This is most likely a byproduct of Semiotics (and Visual Sensory Processing?). The presenter must form the presentation so as to provide information that does not exist encoded within the visuals. THis is naturally where the next Dimension, unifies these separate forms, into spoken word, that is both interactive and intuitive (depending on the Professor at hand, almost literally.)
    
3. __Narrative Unification:__ The Walkthrough of complex ideas, arranged in a nonfinite, nonlinear space (both the conceptual and the physical medium, are non-linear spaces and are non-finite, i.e., inexhaustible in their capabilities for externalization), the live progression or "unravelling" of Cognitive Externalization, is precisely why this is of such importance; the story several unifying categories and domains/dimensions of information, that are separate, convoluted, or non-present in other forms.

## The difference between "how" and "why"
Once information is externalized from conceptual space, into concretized form, a translation and synthesis has already occured. That particular synthesis is merely one such stage however, but it is one of the most difficult. Externalizing and explaining how things come to be (easily verifiable via formal proof, Ontology,  Logic, etc.) is completely different from "why" things come to be. It's the age-old theological argument; What is vs. What ought to be.

This is exactly what Frankl stated in his incredible life's work: "He how has a Why can bear any How." 

The problem of Information (and the obsession of), may be explained by the reversal: "You cannot extract an 'Ought' from an 'Is', or a 'Why' from a 'How.'"

2. Indirect

## Key idea
What ought to be, appears to be a problem not for information of the material, apparent world (facts, data, rules, relationships, laws, co-relations, etc) but for two distinct phenomenological realities:
- Phenomenology: The world as we experience it
- Dream: The world we imagine it could be, it is, should be,
- Structure: The world as we remember it (memory), the world as a coherent framework (worldview), the world as it trends (prediction)
