One of the biggest challenges we face is, how complete do we want to make the world?

It is not merely a density of information problem; it is a richness... a vividness of life... and life is the reference... so we must go to the original source material to understand our problem.

A useful systems theory in this case is the "Distribution Graph/Invariants of Alpha and Omega" which states **"There is actually a ceiling to how complex things can become, before a system is unstable."**
