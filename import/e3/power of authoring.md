The power of authoring literature, especially that of fiction, is not that we are telling a story; but that we can inhabit the characters... though we are limited physically, through imagination; __anything is possible.__ This is precisely why we need constraint and form to harness the power: and the narrative power of being able to speak "through" a character, is precisely what a great actor must feel when they inhabit a role... this is actually the prime function of the Imagination; of Dreams. To explore the unknown, and extract solutions to the problems we face here.
