Age: Aeons

Bio:

A Swiss/German doctor, inspired heavily by the late Swiss Psychoanalyst "Carl G. Jung". This doctor however, is half machine, half man (not cyborg, but a "Bio-automata"). Inspired by Viktor's new rework from League of Legends and Arcane. His name is "Tobias" as Human form, now rebuilt himself as "Tobi, Version X, Model C", symbolizing his incomplete state. His life can be summarized as: "the only constant of the universe is change." Constant and Change are both words that begin with "C" which is also the notation for "Constant" in Physics as "C"

It is also the letter of a programming language, and it is the third letter of the English alphabet. A trinity. Therefore, Tobi is split into 3 parts: Machine, Human and Will.

The Human is the Emotional the Feeling, the Will is the Force of the engine, and the Machine is the body of the Engine, the pure cold hard logic. These are the "Universal Bodies of Constant" or in "Tobi's" language, "The Constants."

We should incorporate this into the concept art sheet. However that may fit.

The artstyle should be somewhat realistic with light motifs of fantastical elements, like that of DMC5 (80% Realism, 20% Effects). Additionally, the pose and setting, should not be post-apocalyptic (it is too cliche for the cyborg theme), instead, the theme is that of a cosmos, and that of Human psychology; Psychological restoration and rehabilitaton. As such, the cyborg "Tobi" is something of a Cosmic Existential Automata: The Human will and intellect, personified in Machine form.

"Man is of instinct, but also of machine. Emotion, energy in motion, is the fuel of Man. But reason and intellect, is the actuator. The spark of the motor, is the combustion of the motions of energy (Emotions and Feelings, typically Motivation), but the force of sustenance, is the will itself."
