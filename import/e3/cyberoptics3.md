# Epistemic-Spontaneity
This foundational theory attempts to explain the Phenomenology and Sytsems Science as to why 

**"Why can't I just focus on what's most important?"**



**"You cannot optimize the world, nor plan perfectly, nor reach perfection, nor understand anything entirely that is detached from it's embedded relata."**

The reason we need Telos in Cyberoptics & Cyberneering, is becuase we do not know the perfect or linear path from 'a' to 'b'. There are several details that are only visible, when on-field; impossible via other methodology other than spontaneous contact. This is the principle of "Epistemic-Spontaneity"

In other words; this is precisely why we cannot force ourselves a perfect destination, path, list, or methodology; there is simply too much we do not know, and what we do know, there is simply too much that we cannot afford to stop and observe and complete (Continuity over Completion)
