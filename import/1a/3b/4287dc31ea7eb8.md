## Principle 5: Keep the machines away from the Dinner Table
"If you measure the world by discrete parts, you will shrink your lens to tiny worlds." This is useful as described in Cyberoptic Oclussion, but for most of everyday life; keep the Machine away from the Dinner table.

Balance.

Keeping everything in balance is not just another job in your calendar, it's a necessary pre-condition for your Sanity; Your Internal and External Biota.

We simply cannot keep track of the variables: there are too many.

This is why we sample. This is why Organic Growth is sustainable despite Imperfect Conditions and the natural decline of Entropy.

No matter how perfect you try to be; you are never going to be perfect enough, nor are you going to feel as if you are "enough." It is a phenomenon of the Human Capabilities... we are aware of what we cannot reckon with; awareness of our limitations... and yet... how are we able to do anything if there is so much outside of us, that we are not in control of?

This is where the Principle of Scale arrives: Macro/Micro. Let's apply this example to an illustrator; there are two dimensions of problems here. 
1. Physical Machinery: The illustrator lacks knowledge, experience or ability of the Mechanics involved in the General Illustration Process. (How to mechanically transfer Imagination/Subject into Illustration, How to move the brush effectively, etc.) This is the level of Techne or Technique.
2. Theoretical Programming: The Illustrator lacks knowledge of Theory.
