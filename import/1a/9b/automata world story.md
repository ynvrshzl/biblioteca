# The Brain/Machina/Automata of Babel

Automata world

Rust/Automata hypothetical thought experiment and Philosophy of some of the biggest problems in our times: Concerning the Dualism between Robotics (And General Technology) and the Disturbing rise of Psychological Disorders and Stress

"Imagine a world in which Death, Disease and the Insufferable aspects of Human Life, are simply a memory of an age of the past. Perhaps, that memory and even history itself, time itself, can be erased. We no longer carry the burden of our Human Form, for we are as Machines, the Automata has Liberated us."

A world of perpetual motion.


"What if robots recursively repaired themselves endlessly? A world where even energy"
