War is one of the sources of Human Meaning that is the most Brutal and Honest.

The Dimension of Meaning is often interwoven in Facade: However, the undeniable sources of meaning are those we feel before we see or think.

While no one in their Sane mind would think for a second that War brings anything but Existential Mortal Suffering... Let's remember the other side of War; a Cry for Defense and Dignity

The components of War therefore reveals one of the most important sources of Meaning in Human life; Confronting the Odds and fighting for a Reason. Whether for loving my child, protecting my home, my people... my land... my history... our history... the Object of Reason is the Reason... which we Reason with... we Wrestle with.
