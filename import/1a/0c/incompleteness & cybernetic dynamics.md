Cyberdynamics

Cyberdynamics is a Theory Field which proposes our necessity as Human beings for Movement and Dynamic Feedback to maintain Order, Equillibria and Sanity.

Cybernetic Fields, or Feedback-regulatory Lifecycles. However, this is merely self-feedback; we are concerned with Dynamic Movement and external integration.

###### Proposition
~~Everything~~ The world as we navigate is as Human Beings, is fundamentally incomplete to us, at any given Discrete point. This is a Fundamental Axiom, and one that was incredibly difficult to work through: mostly becuase you can't solve the problem of Knowledge or Information Overload, merely by studying Epistemology of Information or Knowledge: There has to be a Dynamic movement, a thread between these points.

Atleast, if we are concerned with the nature of Knowledge, of Analysis, of Observation.[^1]
