Cybersomatics is an Interdisciplinary Superfield, concerning our Physiology, in regards to Systems and how we Navigate and Process and live with the  Complexities of Life.

Cybersomatics, a Theory on "Phenomenology of Cybernetic Feedback, Tactility, Tangibility and Regulation."

This theory is not fundamentally focused on work, however, the active applicatory nature of work, is a Tangible, Familiar space of reference for this field. Many of these concepts later become interwoven into everyday real practice.

This may explain why writing, reading, organizing, and mundane chore work, often serves as background noise; these activities are almost entirely affordable via Automaticity. However, what happens when the only context exists in this Category of non-feedback work?

Here we try to unify Cybernetic Systems Theory, with Phenomenology of Sound of Body and Mind (Psychosomatically speaking...): How the Human Being functions properly.
