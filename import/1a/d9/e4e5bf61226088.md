The purpose of Theory, at it's most Functional, is not to reduce the gaps in our understanding; but to understand just enough about why things work, to reduce guesswork, and increase ability.

This may even be the purpose of Educational Knowledge, for that matter; to bring forth the faculty of abilities, which are otherwise dormant in the Human Being. As with Human Life, we must gauge it ultimately, against the scale of Ethics, Telos, Purpose, etc.

This does not enforce a Utilitarian world-view; some things can be intrinsically meaningful, or simply not even useful; and this appears to be necessary in Mamallian Biology (If you want to argue from the Lens of a Darwinian.)
