## Density
We often do not realize just how much information we process; even explicit, conscious information. Much of it is often recycled or forgotten hours after processing.[^1] 

Given this Phenomenology, it is not necessarily a vast leap to propose: If we were a little more conscious of just how much we actually process in a segment of our day, how would that impact our capabilities? More importantly; how would it influence or change our sense of Progression?

Forcing oneslf to "do more so I remember more" is not coherent with ergo, nor is it sustainable.

Productivity implies exporting or output; product. While a product is necessary in many areas of modern Human Life, it is overkill for most of the day.
