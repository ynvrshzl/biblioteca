"Differentials"

In Cyberoptics, we ~~measure~~ construct systems usig instrturmtns like Planners, Lists, Hierarchies, Organization systems, Scheamtics, grammar, etc. However, the most fundametnal tool is distance; knowing how 'a' connects to 'b' rather than merely knowing 'a' is related to 'b'.

Distance reveals underlying implicit assumptions: details, not otherwise visible in higher-level Strata. In other words; when we inspect the Distance between goals...

What distance are we measuring? Is it specific parts of a system, plans of a system? Goals?

The distance between 'a' and 'b' are not trivial; these are essential to how we navigate, construct, and ... the Systems we use everyday.

For example, the largest distance in a System is between the Human being and Telos.

Becuase the Telos is intangible, we have to divide it into smaller, tangible parts. This process is translated in Cyberoptics by either: Sloped transitions or Step-wise transitions. 

A sloped transiton is a smooth change between 'a' and 'b' in which the positions of segments between, are proportional to the resolution level of Inforamtion. The change is "implicit" in some sense, but the assumptions are explicit: we must know exactly the position between 'a' and 'b' by the level of resolution.

A Stepwise transition is a hard shift from 'a' to 'b', often resulting in implicit assumptions between vast changes.

In essence, when we plan a system, regardless of medium, information structure itself is measured by distance and sharpness; Detail REsolution is measured how much information is Implicit vs Explicit and how much information is On-topic
