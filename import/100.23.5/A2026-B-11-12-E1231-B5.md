"The Cyberoptic Track: Attention and Continuity"

"Aslong as one knows where they have been (historical, backward, past, reflection, memory, lesson), where they are (current, moment, cursor, now, priority, action, spontaneity) and where they are going (lookahead, telos, direction) there is Continuity."

This suggests our internal Human sense of Time is Linear, though Elementary Objects (e.g., Events, Memories and information) are Non-linear
