"If you don't have meaning in your life; find a problem in the world, worth solving."

This is where I would begin searching for meaning; **it is partly what saved my life.** 

One thing that I have studied, is that all paths eventually lead to two outcomes in the Human Condition: 
- We know Pain and Suffering exist
- We know our time and lives are limited
- We know Trauma
- We know if we Love, it also means we can Lose
- We know that we don't know more than we know
- Evil can often be Intellectually Justified, but Morally Inexcusable

Given these facts about life, either Optimism or Pessimism, remain the only two Options. All other Philsophies try to find a balance between; but these are the Binaries; the Extremes. There is no Attitude we know of, towards life, which is either outside Optimism nor Pessimism; they are Extremeties of Being. Perhaps Trandscendence... though it is a Metaphysical, Philosophical idea... one that is dangerous if Embodied; which is why these are the only Two Extremites that matter to us.

We can choose, despite everything we know of (and our work excavates this to it's very bedrock)
