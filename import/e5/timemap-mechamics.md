## 1 - Reframing the Schedule
The purpose of a schedule is something like a Combined/Unified Telos. Given what we know that Telos is Intangible: A Schedule is a "Meeting place" for otherwise Potentiality-space.

A Schedule is not simply "An Arbiter of Time in my day: A Plan." We should reframe the concept of "schedule" as being an "Atlas of Time"

The purpose of a Schedule is to inform one of the various Dimensions of Telos and Time; The Ontological Dimensions of Unifying Telos (Direction), Space (Potentiality), Time (External Constraint), Energy (Internal Constraint) with Actuality 
- What to do at what time
- What is important right now
- What fits the current energy level
- Cues: Why 'x' fits 'y': What to do when one needs to review/work/exercise/socialize.
