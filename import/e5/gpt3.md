## Phenomenology

The most fascinating difference between the "Tour-like" Zettelkasten and the Open Library format shared by Encyclopedias and Editorial Informaion Systems, is that the Zettelkasten is a System where Navigation is Explicit; the Person is able to explore Relevance by Relativity; Dynamic non-linear flow (which is somewhat similar to the Human Brain.) While Encyclopedias afford a Mass Overview of Knowledge, where Navigation is Implicit.
