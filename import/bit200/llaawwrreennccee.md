
Lawrence's life story is essentially an Allegory of Role models; The importance of our interconnected-humane-influence. His story essentially follows a traumatized child, ripped away from the fabric of everyday life, thrown into adulthood and teenagehood evaporated. Facing the harsh, cold (literally; he nearly dies of frostbite. but the story expresses how these events shaped him into the man he later became.)  realities of Human nature and his devleopment of the "Facade" or the "Depths of the Abyss of the Human Ego and Fallen Nature."

If it were not for his role models; he would have ended up exactly as many who walked the etchings of his life; a machine of equal destruction. "The world betrayed you? It stole your very life? Fine. Destroy the world. Let it all fall apart. But  be willing to watch the very thing you loved, be taken away not only from yourself; but the innocence you trample. Sacriledge." - Lawrence's Musings on Politics and the Divine
