Once a structure becomes dense enough in surface area; it will eventually occupy enough of our linear-focal view that nothing else can be seen.

This is where hyperpositions emerge topology; once a structure can be abstracted into dense-form groups; those groups can be grouped, recursively. This presents a unique problem of power distribution; on the far end of the spectrum, we are increasing our global foresight, while on the other end, we are obstructing internal contents.
