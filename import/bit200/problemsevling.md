Research often introduces explosive-combinatoric-noise, because it exposes the underlying abstractions. This implies two things; research is an instrument of exposing abstractions and understanding systems or problems deeply. Therefore, one must assess if the problem we are facing is Epistemic (Knowledge-based) or if it is Abstract (Conceptual), therefore the second implication is that we are to simplify the model as effectively as necessary. Often, we only truly discover what works and what fails, on-field. The problem is that field-data is expensive; especially if it is Phenomenological, it implies real-world consequences. This is precisely where Research exhibits an "Intellectual Incubator" structure. A form of manipulable symbolic space, before committing to problems in the real world.
