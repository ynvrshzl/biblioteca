In an Age

Based on what I have experienced Phenomenologically; there are perhaps two fundamental kinds of Physiological states that are subjectively more insufferable (perhaps verifiable through surveying & research...) than the Philosophical problem of Suffering and Pain, or even of Human Malevolance. These two states are: "Chronostasis" and "Errata."

Chronostasis is a neutral term; in some sense, Love can be Phenomenologically described as being "timeless." It's ability to transform ephemeral Human experience into memories, is a testament to it's powerful bonding, selfless, transcendent nature. This is the positive axis of Chronostasis. The negative axis is precisely the major problem here

Errata; described as "Erratic. Error. Confusion. Disorientation." This is by far the worst state I know of. These are essentially the constituents of the worst parts of the Human experience; and how we may respond with life, despite everything.[^1]
