# Tenets of the Kosmikon Project
The night prior to authoring this document, I was reading over someone's publically accessible knowledge base. Some of the ideas were genuienly fascinating; and therein lies the problem. Captivating ideas may illuminate as a lightbulb; but this is merely a mimicry of the true source of light; life.

## I
"As we may think, before we may act." Knowledge must be actualizable in real life.

Without actuation, knowledge is power, and power without action is death. Knowledge must directly or indirectly assist in the "kosmonetes"; solution of a problem, contribution of an ethical idea, ...

## II
Knowledge must be structurally fortified.

This applies to organization of knowledge, formatting of documents, and the granular; linguistic presentation of ideas.
