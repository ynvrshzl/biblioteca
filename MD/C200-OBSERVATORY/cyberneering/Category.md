## Base definition
A "Category" is in essence, a group of shared characteristics of items, objects, idaes, people, places or abstract things.

## Functional use-cases: 
Categories are primarily used all throughout the world to Organize Complex Information and Systems.

## Forms
The simplest form of a Category is the abstract component: "Label", "Box" and "Items." The Box is in essence, the boundaries of the Category. While the Label is a compressed unit of information of internal space. The Items are classified such that they exhibit Characteristics and Features within a shared space.
