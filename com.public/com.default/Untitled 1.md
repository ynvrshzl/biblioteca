# Metamoprhosis
During depressive episidoes or moments throughought my days, I often ask  myself "why can't I just switch my behavior to do the right thing?"

That would be cheating oneself of the real battle; becoming, learning, growing, transforming, metamorphosizing.

It would also be incrdibly dangerous if we may act against our current state, identity of reasoning; such an ability would enable us to do great things indeed, but also allow for unexpected harm to sprout from a seed of good intent. Imagine switching by anger; the consequences would be catastrophic.

Perhaps, it is in the pushing uphill, of becoming, that shapes one's character the most... more than fate or destiny; a Telos.
