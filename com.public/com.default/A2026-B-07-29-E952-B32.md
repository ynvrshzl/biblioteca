# Forces in the Youniverse
Have you ever wondered what malevolence would look like if it was captured in body form? would it be a creature? would it be a computer program? I think malevolence, is a dynamic (between person, and affected), it cannot merely be captured, but it may be represented in mythological form.

malevolence, tragedy, but also fortune, and blessing, how, phenomenological aspects be represented mythologically? what would be the significance of making visible of these intangible forces?

that is where this story begins! is the youniverse, we understand these forces as the natural forces, or natural principles. consequences of(...)

There are figures that form in the imagination, each are an interpretation. there is no such thing as, the mythological representation of tragedy, for example, rather, we understand that within the universe, there is one of many interpretations, of a very real invariant force. this separates youniverse mythology, from classical mythology, we don't imply there is a physical form to these beings, or that there can be only one interpretation... rather, each persona within their own universe, has their own interpretation, of the many forces within the larger youniverse, which itself, is part of a youniverse; essentially "the author."
