Something that I'm processing recently, unravelling the problem of unjust conditions. 

Alot of people, ase they grow older, become cynical and it is... it is exhausting to be around someone who is complaining all the time. It isnt' merely "victim syndrome" however, often times, we have absolutely not the slightest glimpse into what another human being has survived.

Pain, may be the base mechanic for determining what is real; it is undeniably real. However, the dynamics of everyday life, contain dynamics of unfariness. This however, does not imply life is a series of unfortunate events with an inevitable beginnign and an end; that is the cynic's interpretation. 

How may we productively process and make terms with the conditions of life? What does this code reveal, now about a metaphysical source, but about how one ought to behave?

This is first and foremost, the reason I refuse to explore the metaphysical or theoretical; life is far too short to exhaust ourselves in open-ended webs of argument; a reasonable person must confront the problems we face and refuse...
