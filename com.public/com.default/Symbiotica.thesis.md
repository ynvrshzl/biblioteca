# Symbiotica
Meaning in human life must be recipriocal and exchange of positives: Symbiotic. 

In the most observable, fundamental sense of this idea, is our social nature as human beings. We imitate images of others, wether splendor in quality or justified in putrid.

Trying to connect this to... how we influence others, as they influence us. THat is the fundamental source of meaning. Even a meaning defined in the material, such as a mathmetician falling in love with mathematics, or an artist devoting their life to music... these are forms of life
