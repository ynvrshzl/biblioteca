"Whereas we do not wrestle with physical bodies, but with the unseen forces which bridge all things, and with the finite contract of aliveness, marked by the rusting and decay."

## Phenoma, Phenomos, Phenomena
Ideas are powerful, they can make the difference between someone ending their life, or choosing to stay alive. That was my life, every single day, for most of my teenagehood and adulthood. What's fascinating, is how we orbit around ideas, which fundamentally guide our very lives. These ideas are not merely artifacts of human imagination; just as mathematics is not purely invented. These are symbolic interfaces which allow us human beings to probe at the inner and outermost workings of the world and ourselves. 

That is the fundamental tension, between the world outside, and the self within. At the boundary between these scales, is a unifying force; gravity.

What is this gravity, as we may phenomena understand it? Perhaps, ideas, interpretation, are the very life force which drives our intellectual strengths. The claim is that ideas, presuppositions, and axioms form the most fundamental fabric of the tapestry of our most foundational psyche.
