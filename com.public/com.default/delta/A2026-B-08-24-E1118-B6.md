At the edge between intrinsic and extrinsic, or internal and external, is the boundary of meaning. In other words, a transformation-interface waits for input from the external world, processes such input internally, and returns an output externally. At the boundary between external and internal, is where the interface, phenomenologically exists, at the level of purpose analysis. Without this layer, the interface exists merely as an assembly of parts, functions and dynamics.
