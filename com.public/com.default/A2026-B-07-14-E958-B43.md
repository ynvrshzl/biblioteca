---
title: The sensory system
---

I believe the story of human begins at our sensory system; it is the only sensible place to begin... (this has to be the greatest pun of all time.)

The diverse orchestra of hman sensory systems, can be described as something like "navigational tools for the natural (extrinsic) and intrapersonal (intinrsic) world." 

Our visual optic system solved the problem of vision, and the phenomena of proprioception (our ability to sense our own weight in space) give us the ability to navigate, orient, act, and make decisions in space. This is why the human being is not merely "cognition"... the software that is intellect may require the hardware of the body, to be [useful?]
