# Model of learning
This is fascinating: as human beings we appear to learn via 
- Model: Narrative structure as an axis for the complex multi-dimensional nature of information.
- C: The ability to convert the method into the abstract
- Synthesis: Questions of abstract inquiry into clear method
- Staging: Series of baseline information in chunks
- Sampling: Exploring within a bounded context 
- Orthogonal Mapping: Connecting the dots between the known, and potential known 
- Memory: Abstraction of tacit knowledge into embedded intuition (or muscle memory, consolidation)


## Significance
by understanding how we effectively learn as human beings, by understanding the patterns of learning, the structure of learning, the models of information synthesis... we waste less effort on poorly structured knowledge. access to clear material
