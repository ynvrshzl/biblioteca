Lawrence's little brother died when he was in his teenage yaers. It scarred him so deeply that he questioned "God... why?"

Lawrence's mother died shortly after her youngest son was mur--- in cold blood. She died by heartache. Being so terribly young, Lawrence found himself an old man, stuck in a boys body. What kept him alive? A role model; he looked up to. A figure of justice. A statue in the funeral garden cemetery. The statue was of a man who founded the Atlas of Lost Angelis itself; "...". Lawrence looked up to the statue, and that is where he realized the meaning of his life... "I find myself in a storm. I feel a hole in my heart... How am I still alive? The world has never stopped revolving... but my world has completely fallen. What if, it is the very reason I was born? What if... mother and brother are watching me from above? What if I am the storm that is approaching?"



The only thing that kept Lawrence from committing suicide, by the agony of losing his little brother he loved so much... the only thing that kept him alive was to become so terrifyingly intelligent that he would become a torrent of justice, pursuing criminals and evil-doers, and cleansing the world of it's impurities. "May you open my eyes someday, Lord."
