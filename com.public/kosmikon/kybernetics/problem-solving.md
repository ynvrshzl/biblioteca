# Categorizations, Classes, Types and Domains

Aliases: GPS, Epsilon-netics, Geometrics

There are classes of problems. Identifying class is the first stage.

- **"String:"** non-solveable through pure methodological approach. "String" problems are solved through natural processes, i.e., sampling.
- **"Procedural:"** must be solved through methodological-driven approach. i.e., a multi-stage surgical procedure.
- **"Superset:"** when issues overflow system capacity for... priority emerges as a variable of sustainability.
- **"Chaos:"** problems without form or structure, unsolveable or non-understandable through machine or human means.
- **"Emergent:"** or side-effects of unintended, unpredictable protocols or unexpected behavior or results.
- **"Non-linear:"** a particularly problematic subset of noise and chaos, requires high-levels of human sustained focus and coordinated ability to maintain continuity.
