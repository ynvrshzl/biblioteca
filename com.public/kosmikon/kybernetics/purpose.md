# What is the purpose of this field?
"Do not abandon your mast, for the mysteries of the sea are endless and know no end, but the force of life is that which commands order out of chaos; and the force of good is that which makes all living things."

What is the purpose of Kybernautics? Infact; this is where the field begins; and at a meta-level; where all fields eventually return to or begin. Kybernautics is simultaneously; a commentary of collective human knowledge, and an extensive framework for complex navigation. 

It begins at the level of the question: "What are we doing? What is the purpose of this?" The question is an ontological object of direction, of orientation. 

As one of many authors of this fascinating field; I envision it as integral to daily human life today, as matheamatics and history have been for centuries of Education history. Phenomenologically, the field began when I encountered a storm of chaos; one that simply refused to evaporate from my attention, or... Kybernautics begins at the phenomnological cross-roads between meaning and productivity, energy and exhaustion, survival and life, the meta and the micro: "What am I supposed to do with all of these ideas?" and "How do I prioritize or organize my daily actions so that I am in service of the highest good we can sustainably concieve of?"

This is where Telos emerges;

Why is the storm of my brain of chaos and not of relentless force?
