# Heurithmetics
The problem to solve: how do we effectively compute and calculate quantity and quality, when precise values are not accessible?

## Proposition: "Naturalis"
"Proportion over precision"

Instead of counting time by precise, e.g., "2:30 PM" or "55 Minutes until lunch", we calculate via natural hierarchical, proportional, relative ranges e.g., "Noon by half" or "About a full cycle."

Our ancestors measured time in chunks of around **15** minutes. This naturally slices the hour into four quarters.
## Phenomenological Energy calculation
This language opens the pathway to calculate phenomeoligcal units, that are otherwise too precise mathematically, or too abstract to measure. E.g., "High voltage"

## Context
Context is per individual. "High" may not be experienced equally by two individuals.
