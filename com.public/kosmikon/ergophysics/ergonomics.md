# Ergonomics
**There is an ergonomic strategy to learn and approach many aspects of everyday life.**

While managing complex projects, scheduling my day, and brain-fog, I realized something fascinating; there is a correct way to do things. There is an ergonomic way to schedule your day, to learn subjects, to read.

This is essentially how we learn mathematics, and even how mathematics was constructed; as a tiny set of ever-increasing additive complexity. This is becuase it follows the design of neuro-ergonomics


If a class of action, usually work or subjects, or even learning itself &mdash; feels extraordinarily difficult, most likely a sufficient strategy is not implemented. An algorithm is a strategy for computational problem solving, but a logoalgorithm is an algorithm for real-world problem solving

AGE (applied general ergonomics) is a field of study and application of ergonomics; not optimization. It studies strategy rather than efficiency. Organic growth, rather than precise metrics.
