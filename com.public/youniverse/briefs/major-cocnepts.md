# Major concepts throughout the Youniverse

## Section 1.0 - Psyche
Psychological concepts 
### 1.0.1 - Ego
An ego or "the" ego, is the inner-most, natural instinct of the human being.
### 1.0.2 - Persona
A persona is a model of personality; not characterizing a human being, but one of many personalities; a person can exhibit multiple persona; multiple personalities.

### 1.0.3 - Self 
The self is the integrated person; the exexcutive personality


### 1.0.4 - Shadow 
Jungian concept, to describe the bottom-most... occluded phenomenologies of the person.
