# History of the "E" Dream engine
**"What if my life could be edited to erase all pain?"**

"What if I could become anyone I imagined?" She thought to herself, as the blue skies and green trees washed over her troubles, manifold.

A scientist in Metropolis thought to herself, as she rode an old freight train down the countryside. She experienced such happiness, it was almost as if she was living in a completely different world, with a completely different body. All of her problems vanished underneath the blue skies. The green hills and dense trees captivated her imagination.

It was then that she realized "Human imagination... dreams... emotion... physiology... these must be the answer to the invisible human condition..." She began sketching ideas on such an invention. Without a clue where to begin, she removed all walls, and allowed her imagination to flow freely like the rivers of the country. 

With this freedom however, came a haunting warning; **the human imagination is capable of many things, but editing the fabric of life should not be one of them.**

The "E" engine was capable of generating any human emotion within a vivid dream. The dream was selected based on the individual's genetic code and memory, amongst other complex processes. Ensuring the experience captured and captivated the individual, not merely entrancing. 

The genetic clockwork of the machinery was so immensely complex, nobody understood how it functioned internally; only that it produced perfect dreams, without error.

It was reported that 9 out of 10 people would become incapable of exiting the dreams; not that they confused it with reality, but that it was so captivating, they did not want to return. 

She ended up recreating that very same train ride, only as a dream world, "Nonaxphyxia" or "Non asphyxiation." She believed we "suffocated" underneath our suffering, trauma and unprocessed materials. **"Just as the body must digest unused material, so shall the soul."**
