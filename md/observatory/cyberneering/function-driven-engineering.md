# "Function-driven Engineering (FXE)"

## Abstract
In [Cyberneering](md/observatory/Cyberneering.md) "Function-driven-engineering" is a core principle, precisely due to it's foundational effects on the scaling of a System.

## Principle
In essence, the principle states "Any feature, decision or part introduced without a clear purpose, reason, solution or function, will inevitably increase system overload."

In this case, "system-overload" refers to [system-overload-entropy-princple](md/observatory/cyberneering/principles/system-overload-entropy-princple.md)

However, as powerful as FXE may be, it also needs to be carefully balanced to avoid ["Information-precision-overload"](md/observatory/cyberneering/principles/information-precision-noise-overload.md)
