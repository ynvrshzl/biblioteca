---
title: "Worldview, Value Theory, Attention and Human experience."
---

What we pay attention to, is not merely Phenomena; but "Phainos" or "That which appears forth." In a natural scene, this is most essential; as our attention exhibits internal biases (and other complex...) to look in certain directions, and not others...

It appears that how we experience the world is based primarily on "Domains" of the Human being. These Domains (subconscious, memory, conscious, etc.) contribute to the momentary experience at any given moment in Time; such that no Domain is isolated; but part of the Continuity that is Experience. It matters to know how we experience life differently, not for philosophical or Scientific reasoning; but so that we may solve the most difficult, unmapped phenomenological problems we face today.
