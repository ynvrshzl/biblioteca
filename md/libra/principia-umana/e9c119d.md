What does it mean to be Human?

Those are the first words that came to mind when I looked into my own eyes; there is so much we simply will never know.

I think we should begin at the end of all things; the fact that we are limited by time and not by space. Everything has an end; but a Human life is the most tragic ending of all (separate discussion.).

What does it mean to be Human? How should a Human being behave? How should a person respond to both extremes; Life's greatest gifts and most brutal of suffering? Is there an answer that awaits even in the most insuferrable of living conditions? When is it too late?

This literary investigative work aims to present a unification of the most important Philosphical, Mythological, Sacred Frameworks with real-world stories of people whom have survived and returned with evidence; **Life is worth wrestling with.** "Israel" in the Hebrew-Sacred-traditions, translates to **"We who wrestle with God."**

It is structured via inquiry; questions which reveal the path unknown; Quests. Sometimes however; we don't even know what are the correct questions to ask. This is where the work..
