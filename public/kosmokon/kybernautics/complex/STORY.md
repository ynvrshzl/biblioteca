The model of a story

A story tells history through compressed points of information, or ["Kybnetes"](Public/E100-KOSMOKON/10-KYBERNAUTICS/KYBNETE.md) These points form topology, which si the basis of how we navigate informaton, and how sytsems process information.

At the bassis of computers is not mathemaitcs; but information and how to manipulate information. This is the basis of logic, and mathematics as numerical logic.


Evolution is a story about change over time; it proposes 3 major Information Kybnetes: Information about change itself, Information about the past version, Information about the current version.
