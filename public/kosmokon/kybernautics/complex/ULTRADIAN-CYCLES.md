90 - 120m cycles of natural chrono

I am developing a two-fold theory on human well-being. For the first part of the theory, I believe these cycles are crucial for focus and timing work. For life outside of work, I believe these cycles establish the structures of perception; which may be the uncharted waters of the phenomenological; a core essence of my work.

Albert Einstein understood, through his work on General Relativity, time is influentiable by the shape space; supermassive celestial bodies curve spacetime, and ... but it is also relative to a local context. This is fascinating when connecting to our perception of time, becuase it is one of the only things we can somewhat influence, yet it is not primarily in our conscious grip, and it's source of perception; time, is most certainly not in our control... or is this truly the case? This is where time perception becomes influenced by General Relativity; given a local time space across two points, two people may experience time entirely differently.

Time is perhaps, the fundamental instrument of order in living systems; a clock, per se. It's fascinating how this is implemented at the genetic, chemical substrates of life; where time is not measured precisely, but as rhythym. That may be our natural sense of time; cycles, rhythyms, tempo, pace. Not precise numbers.

I believe, however, we cannot ignore the moral responsibility of being integrated into society; and this involves precise time-keeping.
