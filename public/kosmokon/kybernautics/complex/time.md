A fascinating discovery during Kybernetics studies; for polymaths/neurodivergent individuals (and otherwise, individuals expressing the same neuro atypical-ness) there are two fascinating propositions I have observed from extensive phenomenological research;

- The natural source of energy is not priority it is recency/salience
    
- This same dynamic is responsible for their natural source of focus, and effective resource management.
    

For context, I proposed the idea of "organic economics of resources." Things like attention, self-control, discipline, pain tolerance, endurance, focus, complexity management, etc. Amongst these growing observed resources; time is one of great importance. It cannot, however, be isolated from energy; which directly influences our sense of (perception of?) time.

This is tremendously useful becusae it means; we do not prioritize linearly; we work at an organic, creative pace.

Apart from deep analysis, what is conclusive appears to be; captured in the vine analogy; the balance between open-ness (space?) and control;

- non-tyrannical order
    
- just enough structure for growth and creativity to sample and emerge
    

the polymath/neurodivernt individual cannot merely "linear optimize" everything.
