A classical problem in Kybernautics is "The... problem." Essentially, you are assigned a scouting mission; from within an ever-growing library, you have to decide what will be finished, what deserves attention, and how to structure it. No rules, strategies or instructions are provided for you; you are merely scouting an endless forest of knowledge.

This is infact, where Kybernautics itself began as a study; not as an intellectual field of knowledge; but as a probing enviornment for the study of real-world problems governing information.

The fact that these articles hold potentially life-saving knowledge, immediately shifts the lens from "managing some files" to "information has a weight, yet it is fundamentally unmeasurable."
