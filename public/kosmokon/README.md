# Cosmicon

## Lore and Myth
"The Kosmokon..."

The Human-Brain may be considered a Cosmis object; a "Kosmokon." Requiring deep-fine-tuning and discipline to unlock it's full capacities for power and Ethics. Fascinatingly; the brain is not self-sufficient. This si evident when a person is isolated in an empty room; madness ensues. If the brain was self sufficient, it should have the ability to self-sustain indefinitely; however, this is absolutely not the case. We are as much embedded in the Cosmos... as it is a reflection in the Human-Being. Imagination in some sense, is this model.

"Within the fabric of the cosmos, exists all solutions to all problems. There is a solution to every problem, it is found in life, not in death."

Naturally this exposed a tunneling of inqury; Questions such as "What about euthanasia? Isn't that a problem to a solution?" 

## The physical Kosmicon
The idea of the "Kosmodex" or the "Kosmikon" is most accurately materialized as a piano; there exists within the piano a nearly infinite set of possible sounds; but only very few of those combinations produce beautiful music. 

Freedom is not born of either Chaos or Order, but by Law or Bounded Order; Where Chaos becomes an agent, ratehr than a Corrosive.

Not only does masterful arrangement of music, result in structural beauty; but it simultaneously reduces pain (By integrating that which would be "chaos" in isolation.)

As such, the idea of a "knowledge biblioteca observatory" is merely one body of the work; just as a piano itself is not the music, but an instrument. 

As such, to instrumentalize knowledge, is to use knowledge as power in actuality.
