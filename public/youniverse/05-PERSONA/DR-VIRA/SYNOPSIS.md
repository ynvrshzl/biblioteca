Dr. Vira's life becomes embedded is one of twofold; chaos consumes her life, and she's battling with the very nature of evil and pain.

Her studies become an analysis of life itself "Why are we here? Why is the world such an unfair place? Why do some suffer underservingly so... and others do not? Why is pain so horrifying it makes you want to die, and love is so beautiful it makes you want to live? What is this madness?"

She defines the unintelligible nature of chaos and order, by "cryptos" a labyrnth stretching the human mind, the world, and the interlocking systems of clockwork and phenomena; she basically looses her mind trying to "develop an algorithm to solve the cryptos."

Vira almost dies trying to "understand reality and the cosmos" after reading great literature on physics, science and the giants of recorded history... "I want to be like them... I want my name and image preserved and reveered."

This ego of Vira, was detrimental to her health; pushing herself far beyond the human capability.

Dr. Vira eventually realizes a terrifying reality; her brain is both incredibly powerful, and existentially incapable. More powerful than any computer, yet exhausted

Dr. Vira eventually becomes a psychologist; integrating her analytical prowess with her newfound source of meaning; perhaps the very source of life itself. "To be in service of others."

The fascinating part is when her life found order; not by her engineering of it, but by her service of a greater good. "This must be how you designed the cosmos..." one of Vira's most favorite phrases, alluding to her belief in a designer of the cosmos; Elohim. Her expansive knowledge of mathematics, science, computers and technology.

One of the most painful parts of her life is when she realized that we come to this life, completely naked, terrified, hungry and helpless; as we grow up we develop our shells of naivety; child-like innocence. We simply experience life for the phenomena; arguably the glory of Eden as it was designed; to experience it without self-awareness.
