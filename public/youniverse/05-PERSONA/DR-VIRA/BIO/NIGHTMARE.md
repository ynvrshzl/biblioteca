Dr. Vira's nightmare essentially... she sees people as mannequins or lifeless shells; and it's so petrifying it's essentially her own psycho-hell. The worst part is the general-mundaneness, and the sacred nature of life. Children and Families suffer, and are suffocated; they have no voice. The wealthy have smiles but do not have eyes to see, nor ears to hear. 

She looks at herself and realizes there is blood on her hands.

Off in the distance a raven-like plauge doctor, witch-like figure points towards Dr. Vira. It says nothing; it's silence is Ominous enough.

Her life accomplishments burn and evaporate to the soil; the ashes.

"Why are we so empty inside?"

She eventually converts her life to Hinduism and the pursuit of truth. "We are as Kaleidoscopes, each form a different point of view, casting mirrors from a ray of light, but there can be only a single ray of pure light."
