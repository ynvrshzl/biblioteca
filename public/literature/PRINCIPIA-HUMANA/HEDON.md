Why is Hedonism harmful to Human Life?



When I indulge in the bodily sensations, beyond what is necessary, I feel stick to my stomach; knowing I am contributing next-to-nothing to an already profoundly sick society.

Yet, I also must acknowledge; we must acknowledge, that no person is sanctified or freed from our carnal form; this body has the blueprint of either a remarkable architecture, or an isolated-labyrynth.

Equillibria, is a grounding principle;

I believe it is precisely that element that makes this so difficult; on one hand it feels amazing, on the other, it's an indulgent disgust; of the most depraved, liberal Human-kind. It has deep significance with the "Sexual Revolution" and it's promised "Liberality" or "Freedom." However, the Freedom to do "Whatever you want with whomever you want" will 100% reach a terminal of unfathomable torment; knowing you contributed nothing to the already profoundly sick society we live in. 

Freedom, is most observable in Music; where highly specific rules emerge a near endless array of possibility and variation. This is crafted freedom. The other, is wreckless and Etropic.
