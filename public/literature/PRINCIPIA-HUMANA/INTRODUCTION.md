The "Facades" of everyday life, are not to be taken for granted. It is precisely this fleet of armory that sheilds our sensitive Human-ness from the Totallity of Essential-or-Elementary-Reality; "The Arch-Logos."

There are things we simply cannot percieve; and most of life, we simply do not percieve. Yet it appears; these "missing-data" are not essential to being Human

We may experience this in either; Severe-Trauma, Selfless-Kindness or Unexplainable-Miracles.

I believe, deep within the bottom-most bedrock of the Human-Condition, is a desperation to know the Logos; to find something which stabalizes the fragmented Psyche. 

Each betrayal, each forgotten memory, every unanswered question, every cry unheard, all the wasted time, and all the suffocating stress, pain, trauma... all the moments we believed we would not survive... all the moments we believed it would have been better to have never existed... these are not neutral emotions, sensations or arbitrary-data; they are alike to Physiological-Wounds; Mending requires process by Healing. What can mend the ... of  a Memory?
