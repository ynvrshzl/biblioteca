# Una
*To dream of a life that is worth living, and to learn to discern what is good from what is not.*

The primary principle in this body of work, is motivated entirely by the study of what is good, and the discernment of what is not.

This appears to be **the most important** human ability; to distinguish what is good from what is not, what is posion and what is life, what is true and what is false, what is right and what is wrong.
