# Modernia: An investigation of modern human life

## Prologue
Suffering is perhaps the most phenemoenologically under-explored domain of the modern human life. We have mapped the stars, witnessed the bottom of the sea, but yet at the depths of the human experience, is a space so largely unmapped, it may explain much of the additional suffering we experience today.

THe magnitidue and depth of psychological exhaustion one may experience today, as a byproduct of miraculous societal growth, is also an area we must not ignore.

The suffering behind closed doors, financial stress, judicial injustice, familial cobwebs, societal pressure, undiagnosed health, staggering medical bills, unsolved crimes. What may we do with all this weight? How may we respond? That, is the groundwork of this survey; a charting into the phenomenological.

## Abstract

The study begins with a breakthrough problem... "Why is modern life so disproportionately distributed? Why do some people suffer so criminally behind closed doors, whilst we undoubtedly posess the necessary infrastructure to feed and clothe everyone?"



Naturally, this discussion seeps across the Cynical, Nihilistic, Existential territories; so I attempt to center on Hedonism as a calibration for these ideas; in a more productive lens, using Hedonic principles (pain and pleasure) as fundamental, indisputable elements of phenomenological reality, and more importantly; How we may respond, properly, as people, to this complex social arachnoweb (a model i'm describing for complex phenomenological issues); 



Where nobody appears to posess a plan, or model of social restructuring... while those are more sociopolitical ideas, the purpose of this study is to productively investigate how a single person should conduct oneself in today's world.



Given the heart of series, we explore Hedonism; the belief that the greatest and most correct pursuit of human life, should be that of maximizing pleasure, and minimizing pain. 



I present 6 arguments on the catastrophic insufficiency and effects of Social Hedonism, and additionally; a story of Hedonism, and why this discussion is more significant to be had today, than it was at it's historical conception in ancient Greece. The positive applications of concepts of Hedonism (generally shallow lifestyle, but some ideas are useful if integrated.)



## A proposed explanation of today's conditions

We may not realize it, but much of the unjust conditions of modern day human life, is by the afterimage of a net of once, sane philosophies and schools of thought. Mostly born out of Greece and other civilizations, eventually pathologized into a careless lifestyle. We explore how this perpetuates the very suffering Hedonism attempts to minimize, and how we may properly respond instead.



## Central Arguments

1. Perpetuation: Hedonism perpetuates suffering, not merely by productive inaction (which is a default consequence of not acting dilligently, in general), but by feeding the imbalance of the world and starving resources by those who need it. (This is the most catastrophic, undeniable effect of Socio-Hedonism.), These effects become observable as injustices in the world we live in, feeding cynicism, nihilism and other forms of destructive philosophy-driven lifestyles. Despite posessing unimaginably powerful models and technology; primitive issues such as starvation and judicial inequality (amongst other socioeconomic issues.) persist today. These issues are largely unnoticed by the wealthy; and largely, they are indirectly, yet hierarchically responsible for the suffering of millions of people and forms of life; by Hedonic, self-serving behavior. Again, this is the most catastrophic point of Hedonism. It does not imply Hedonism is the only source of cruelty and injustice (pathology and psychopathy inlcuded...), but it does keep the study centered on observable slices of the whole; so that we may formulate a cohesive response, for everyday human life.
2. Localized: Entirely dependent on local sense of pleasure. This is a finite, exhaustive, sensitive resource, and biologically; pathways shared with pain itself. It is ironic, to say the least.
3. Insufficient and Incomplete: Does not present a full or accurate model of the world and of human life; failing to integrate both; the tragic conditions of suffering, and the human moral responsibility to confront it willingly.
4. Incongruence; Meaning is not the same as pleasure; even if those inter-sections often reveal in positive emotion towards goals (we may study this separately.) Viktor Frankl's life is evident of this, through the most tragic of lenses. By this same vein of inquiry, Hedonism is not a designer alternative to meaning; the only force that justifies the conditions of life, and sustains the finite human being against the non-finite, is the voluntary, uncondtional participation of life. (...This is a paraphrasing of Frankl's life's work.)
5. Fragile. Hedonism does not prepare oneself for tragedy, nor does it strengthen the structure of local community to withstand the conditions of life. (...this is a deep section, we should emphasize eventually...) The human being is both; biologically fragile yet willfully resillient. Hedonism isolates our fragility and maximizes our helplesness; yet society did not develop that way.
6. Cybernetically incomplete. From the previous point, Hedonism only exists in part of developed society. Hunter gatherers actually did exhibit hedonic lifestyles; but nowhere near the distribution of today's wealth. Hedonism is Cybernetically incomplete when analyzed from the moral domain; it does not contain sufficient resources for the exchange of values or service outside the localized, isolated cluster of pleasure-seeking agents. In other words; Hedonism is synonymous with free-radicals; self-serving to their own code, regardless of the larger structure of being. Which is obviously dangerous. Especially at social scale.



Summary: drawing a normal from these arguments; Hedonism fails precisely at the boundary where philsophies and lifestyles confront the very nature of life and reality itself; no (sophisticated) system can persist as a consequence of single-channel optimization. Even at the biological, mechanical, cyber levels of analysis; isolated optimization dies against the multi-factorial eviornment. That's a brutal blow to the idea that "we can just be happy, and the pain will go away."

## The price of the Hedonist

There is a price to be paid for prioritizing self gratifying behavior. 



Hedonism would make sense in an isolated community, where the avoidance of pain would naturally leave room for pleasure. That makes sense from a logical, biological (notice the "logic" root words in these terms.) lens of analysis; however, the human being is neither; an entirely logical creature, nor a purely instinctual creature. 



When we mis-use our remarkable human-defining abilities (intelligence, problem solving, reasoning, abstraction, personality, etc.) not only does the larger scale society pay a price for this perpetual indulgence; but one's conscience will undoutedbly either crash or become tormented.
