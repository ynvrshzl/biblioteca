Perhaps at our most fundamental nature, we...

What the self wants is what is real, yet we shelter the self from Reality. Why is this the case?

The irony and tragedy of Human-Nature, may be our tug-of-war between the Sacred and the Profane. Within the Mundane, we build walls of Facades. We shield our senses from what is true; for it weighs too heavily on the Conscience. Yet, this part is dying to live; for truth, painful as it may be. It may prefer what is real and pain, than what is false and comfortable; for Human life is already ultimately stricken by suffering, regardless of our choices; a truncated ending; none can escape the accelleration of time. 

Such a Fate may be cruel; indeed, yet cruel is to live in a world, never having contributed to the lessening of one another's suffering through a shared Ethos.
