What would genuinely speak to my younger self? She concluded that injustice and suffering was unanswered in all areas of being; she concluded it was more safe to die than to continue living. The keyword here is safety; I had developed serious agoraphobia. After confronting the trauma I had experienced years ago, I realized there was still no answer; that is another component, an "answer." 

Jordan B. Peterson gave a TedX talk that saved my life. Within this speech, he did not directly address the Phenomenological issues I was confronting in isolation; he attempted to define the social cause and how we define "real." It was the most callibrated balance between mystic oddity, and physical structure; it spoke to me in a way neither side could.

This is how the speech is to be guided. By those principles; that someone  may be watching, who was as broken as I, but through the example of another human being; you realize...
