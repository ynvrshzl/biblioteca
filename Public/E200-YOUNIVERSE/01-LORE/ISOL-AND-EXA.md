# Isol and Exa
"She is the iris and I am the fovea, we need each other to see what the other cannot."

Isol and Exta where one of the original Mothers of the Moons in the Youniverse. They guarded the children in the moons and helped guide each starseed.

Isol and Exta are named derived from "Isolation" and "Extraversion" respectively. "Introversion" and "Isolation" are synoymous throughout the Youniverse.

## Task
The tasks of the mothers were to guide each soul within their respective cluster of "The Spectra Universalis" ...G

Isol examines the "fate" of the rivers of life. Her tears form the river, and she cries for all that is beautiful: "These children come to the world and are alone... some are miserable during their time... How may we protect them from the ego... and from the world?" Exa responds to her sister: "Isol, you cannot intervene with the unravelling and becoming. These are natural principles of the cosmos."

Isol and Exta eventually fought in eternal war; One for power, and the other for mercy.
