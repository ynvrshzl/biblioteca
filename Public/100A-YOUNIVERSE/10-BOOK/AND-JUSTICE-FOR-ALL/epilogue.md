"I know understand why  we cannot solve all problems... we are fundamentally limited... fundamentally... and we must be..."

"For the mind attempts to decipher the mysteries of the design... my friends... the design is the mystery."

"We are not trapped in worlds; we must lead by example."

"The greatest problem is that of which we cannot see, cannot remember, cannot hear. We cannot confront the totallity of all things; for we are mortal; we were designed for small steps in a lifetime. We cannot know all things..."
