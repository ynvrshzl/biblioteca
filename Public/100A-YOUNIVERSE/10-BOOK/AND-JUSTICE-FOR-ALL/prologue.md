# "AND JUSTICE FOR ALL."
The life of Lawrence "Law" Clarkson.

## Chapter 1: "Reconnaissance"

"On his day of birth, the skies were a storm."

"There is a storm approaching."

"What are you going to do about all the people that suffer, who's names we'll never know." Lawrence's eyes gape wide open... the entire court-room collapses into pure silence. Lawrence can hear his own heartbeat, and his slow gulp of guilt... "I... I..." His face starts streaming with tears before he can even realize...

"This may be the only true sin... our failure to act... I believe if one... does all that they can... giving everything they have in this life..."

"The problem with pain is that it rots into cynicism and nihilism. One believes the pain will last forever; and when it comes to an end, one will simply forget it."

"There is, perhaps no problem greater than that we cannot see, cannot hear... cannot remember... cannot study... cannot understand..."

Lawrence cries. "Am I a fraud? ... I could not answer that... no matter what I tried..."

"We are human, Lawrence. We can only do our best."

"You musnt't shoulder the weight of the world like Sisyhpus... or Serena... she died loving too deeply" Lawrence responds petrified: "SHE DIED BECUASE WE LIVE IN A CRUEL WORLD." 

"Then let it be so. This world is cruel. It is broken. It is falling apart. Who may restore the broken, if not the doctors? Then let it be us who shoulders the weight of humankind. May we die? Death!? Tell me Lawrence, the sign of a true man is not intellect, but the fear of life; that there exists problems and evil greater than death itself."

"But death is the only true problem we cannot solve..."
