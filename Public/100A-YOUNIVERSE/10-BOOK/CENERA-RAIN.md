Cerena walks in the rain as she comes home from the funeral of one of her patients.

She describes sadness and trauma, malevolence and evil as stepping into another world... where even the clouds occlude the light above, our right to dream; our birthright. She feels powerless and exhausted, but she knows her responsibility as a caretaker. She looks above and simply closes her eyes, as the rain falls all over her. 

"Lady Cerena, It's raining! You musn't be outside without a coat!"

Rain. The city always rains. Over the walls I can see the skies are clear and the sun always shines, but the clouds above are oh so high.

"Father... do you see your children crying?"
