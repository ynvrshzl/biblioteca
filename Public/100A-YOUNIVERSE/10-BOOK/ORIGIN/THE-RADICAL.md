The Radiasts were obsessed with productivity, work and human excellence. They crafted grand undercities, buried deep beneath the Earth, believing they could harness the natural source of all energy; heat. They proposed societies powered by the labor of the human being; "The sodium in your sweat, the carbon in your lungs will bring about a better world for all!"

Throughout the mega-cities, we see remnants of futurism; advertising in an old age. "Say no to chaos!"

They constructed isolated infrastructure, to harness natural human byproduct.
