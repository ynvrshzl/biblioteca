If semantic-versioning is such an important part of modern software development, why is it such an ambigious specification?

Engineering and Architecture benefit from Schematics. Software is largely a dynamic, ongoing process.

Today, semver may reveal a large gap of software-engineering; between the engineer and the software.

Most of the engineering aspect of software is often expriential knowledge; not standardized in the design of software itself. This would be unacceptable in any other technological field, much less that of engineering.

As a counterbalance however; engineering is unlike mathematics, in that, a large part of the software-engineering process is creative and abstracted; whereas mathematics requires deep theory and understanding of material. Software is something like "fluid" knowledge & creativity, while Mathematics is "stepped."

This is a complex issue, but I hypothesize at the level of tooling; specifically the IDE, software-engineering needs ergonomic, workflow, disciplinary improvements! It is one of the most significant industries and technologies today, yet it remains as an outlier of engineering.
