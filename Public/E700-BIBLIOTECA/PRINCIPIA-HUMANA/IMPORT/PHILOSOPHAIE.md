## 1.0 - Main argument
What if there is no argument against logical unfalsifiability?

Let's assume for a moment the rationalists are correct. That there is "no such thing as free will" or that "we live in a pre-determined universe." Even more destructively, let's assume no argument against highly-dangerous philosphies and ideas, e.g., "sollipsism" and "nihilism." Let's assume there is no free will, or that nothing can be proven to be real or falsifiable outside of the self; I may ask you **what exactly does that change?**
