# Equillibrium as a fundamental function of systems

Balance is perhaps, the most challenging demand of systems, especially the human being.

Our time is finite, our abilities are constrained, yet possibilities, ideas and materials are nearly boundless; nonfinite, indefinite, without end.

This is where prioritization, organization, management, and general kybernetics principles appear as solutions to the nonfintie problem. However orientation, direction and navigation are significantly more foundational and manageable than optimization alone; as it is too, a near non-finite, indefinite space.

## Applied Equillibrium

To balance metrics in a deterministic machine, quickly becomes a demanding task. But managing the various domains in human life, requires something beyond calculation and management... (we explore this evnetually as the heart of the study.)

This equillibrium behaves as a newton's cradle; establishing motion and transfer of mometum across structures. While the metaphor may seem philosophically satiating; it most certainly captures the observable effects of balance in systems.

Feedback and regulation is the Cybernetic perspective of this dynamic; and Cybernetics explores this as a necessary continuation of a system. In other words the cycle influences contunity. Continuity is a central property of equillibrium; especially for the human life. This is perhaps the most important aspect of equillibrium is context; the ability to maintain continuity across scales. This includes the macro and the micro; both are necessary for systems, and for well-being... i.e.,

- Macro analysis of one's life: "Is my family on the right path?"
    
- Micro analysis: "How did my job today reflect in my family?"
