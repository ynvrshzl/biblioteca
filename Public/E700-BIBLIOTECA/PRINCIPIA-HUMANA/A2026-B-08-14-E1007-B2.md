## Autonomy preceeding Biology
Sometimes, When it is applicable, I must not allow room for excuse; but making errors is how we lean as human beings. The biology should not be allowed to act before the autonomy: that is the cardinal sin. 

## Cyclical errors are lessons
If something continously appears throughout time, we know it as patterns. Those patterns hold significance; stable structures across time are clues of an architectural order.

This directly challenges the Futilists world-view, if problems are recurring and siphon our capacity to deal with them, what is left? 

That infact, must be the lesson to be learned. That must be what shapes and defines human character the most.
