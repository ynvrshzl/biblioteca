
# Principia Economica
Just as vines can be encouraged to grow along structural support (like wooden beams), I assume that the Polymath, exhibits an extreme scale of natural human growth, across domains. From intellectual, to productive, to intrapersonal, to psychological... now this is absolutely NOT the same thing as saying "Polymaths are smarter and work faster." If anything, Polymaths and brilliant individuals, often describe their intelligence as something of a burden of immense overhwhelming power... one that cannot simply be ignored or sedated... it must be confronted.

So we naturally diverge into two paths of the intellect; isolated and integrated. What does it cost to be integrated vs. isolated? I believe the largest factor is relativity; isolated intellect works now, not later. (..this is a separate discussion all together...)

Structure, Organic growth, Precision, Creativity. Those are the primary elements of study in Principia Economica: Series on sustainable, ethical, telos-driven productivity. 

For the time being, a basic idea may be drawn from these outlines... "structure is the balance between precision and the unpredicatable nature of organic growth; allowing creativity, decision-making, and higher-order abilities to emerge from the balance between form and function; between precise structures and open spaces."

Back to the model of the vine and the struct; this is applicable phenomenenologically (... i think... becuase we are observing classical mechanics, in organic phenomena)... vines cannot be forced to grow in a narrow structure; conditions of life are not sufficed in such precise order; life needs room to grow. Just as room is necessary; so is structure, once again. Without structure, a mycellial network may die if it merely explores it's space, endlessly, without aim, without goal, without a Telos.

So the goal appears to be smething like an ontological object which unifies the incalculable distance between structure and space; the necessary conditions for organic growth and creativity. Creativity, is perhaps one of the most undervalued, significant functions in the set of human abilities; it allows us to make decisions out of incompete models.

In this sense, we glimpse at the borders of epistemology; what we simply cannot know, what we can't explain, how we act without knowing, and how much knowledge is sufficient for "x" context.

That last problem, is perhaps best undrestood as a metaphor of temperature; every being has their own unique internal sense of "too much" and the physical world will reveal if our knowledge, understanding, and learning, reflects in our abilities, work, actions and being... (...hypothesis, i'm jumping alot of conclusions here...)

So if creativity requires constraint... a balance of space and structure... then the engineering of such conditions is a mirror of creativity; organic growth. We arrive at understanding... atleast... mostly... and this is actually where the picture becomes much more complex; becuase certain abilities are simply not possible if we don't understand them (i.e., surgery or systems engineering), whereas some sitautions are not solveable, purely by intellectual corpus (i.e., socializing, performing CPR, driving, real-time decision-making etc.)

This is a complex web, and I think, it's safe to conclude this as an "undefined" area of study, mostly wrapped in the tacit; the embedded, embodied, abstracted, compressed. Not hidden or unknown; but implicit; relational, relative. (we will explore this separately).

That's most likely the boundaries between epistemology and engineering; what problems can we solve by understanding, and crafting tools? and how does our knowledge limit, support or suppress our abilities to make decisions, orient in space and solve problems? That's most likely why Telos is necessary for human life, even if at an implicit state.

If this model is accurate, then the balance between structure and space is the real task of economica; not mere success or productivity; but effective resource management to be in service of a greater Telos. "How may I make the most of today? Based on what rules, guides, ideas or beliefs?" That may appear as a ghost of psychology; simpler than reality; becuase it must be. Those questions compress an enourmous interwoven tapestry of interconnected parts of life; Organic growth and Creativity appear as the stitching of bridges between those worlds; what we know, what we dont' know, and how we may act and respond. 

"Economica."
