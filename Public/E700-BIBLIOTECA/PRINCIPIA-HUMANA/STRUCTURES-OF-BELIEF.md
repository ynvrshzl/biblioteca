## Defining a belief
What is a belief? Linguistically when we say "I am good this" or "I am bad at this" is synonymous with "I practice the piano" versus "I am a pianist." Proposition: We embody the subject/object as a reason, or purpose for motion.

A belief is a proposition for action or reason; we reason, perhaps to reason about a reason; A purpose for being.

Why purpose, or reason? This may be the fundamental axiom; "They who hath why, may bear any how." A reason allows us to act despite our incompleteness.
