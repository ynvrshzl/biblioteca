# Indefinite Intellectualism
This is primarily, why we cannot merely "rationalize" our way through life as human beings; rationality is endless, life is not.

When we define real things, we describe parts of a whole. When we define what is "real" we are no longer defining real things; we are defining the abstraction of "real." One is a pointer to real phenomena, whilst the other is a linguistic logical inference. This is why it is crucial to avoid indefinite-intellectualism
