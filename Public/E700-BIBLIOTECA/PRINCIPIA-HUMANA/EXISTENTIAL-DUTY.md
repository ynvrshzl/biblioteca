Whenever I'm going for a walk, I see the freight train off in the distance. I hear it's colossal machinery, the tonnes of cargo from all around the world. The does not compare to the weight of the world, crushing one's spirit; especially when one is physiobiotically trapped in an un-ending torturous pain.

As the freight passes by, I think about all of the people who's lives have unfortunately come to an end by it's relentless weight.

Then I realize our existential responsibility.
