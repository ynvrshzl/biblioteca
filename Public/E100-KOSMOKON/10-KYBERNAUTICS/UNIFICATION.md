Why is the unifying of ideas, amongst the most potent of transformations?

Unifying, is an evolved form of the "metaphor" at the level of worlds; not merely phrases.

To make visible of the indivisible or connect the indirect, is to unify. In otherwords; integration. 

A story, unifies across domains, and language, unifies.

Some of the most important ideas were unifying theories; General Relativity, Newtonian Mechanics, Modern Medicine and even Computer Science.
