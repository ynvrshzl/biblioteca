# The significance of the daily rhythm
Cycles, Rhythms, Schedules, Routines, Events. These are not economic objects; they are artifacts of our biology; our own internal time-keeping.

Today, I woke up and was completely lost. "What should I do? Where should I go?" The moment I latched onto order, I got myself out of bed. Almost involuntarily so; "You get your life in order. You can't keep living like this, and you know it."

A personal tenet I have discovered is something like... Do not diverge from the axis of time; no matter how stressful or redundant it may seem.
