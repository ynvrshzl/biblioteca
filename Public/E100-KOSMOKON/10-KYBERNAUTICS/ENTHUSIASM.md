This comes with important caveats; such as Captivation. The subject matter must be intrinsically or extrinsically captivating *not merely entertaining, or organized, or structured or optimized.* The subject or knowledge must integrate the individual's Phenomenology, at the level of engaging captivation; Ideally, Enthusiasm. I think this is primarily what separates the Wheat from the Chaff, so-to-speak; especially in modern endless "internetainment" consumption.

Information of which sparks enthusiasm, is clearly seperate from information that is merely engaging or interesting (the classic interenet dopaminergic chase-and-seek-reward)

I believe it begins at the level of information; of stimuli, awaiting to be processed by the sensory system and integrated into the being. To establish the proper baseline of Education, I believe "Enthusiasm" is perhaps one of the primary variables of sustainable knowledge consumption.
