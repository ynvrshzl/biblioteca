# Kybernautics
Did you know? A "Kybernaut" is an individual who embarks on the life-long Odyssey of bringing order to the maelstrom of Chaos.

## Section 0.0

We envision this field to be as essential as the Mathematics and Science fields of the Education and Academic systems of today. Most especially due to the recent explosion of information and catastrophe; no system, wether mechanical, animal or human, can survive in Chaos, Disorder or Directionlessness. That illustrates the heart of the field, and all motivations branching.

As such, all ideas, models and studies proposed in these series of studies, must in some way, trace back productively to the heart of the matter. [Read more about the guidelines here]()
