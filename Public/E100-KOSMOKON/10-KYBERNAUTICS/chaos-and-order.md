We make sense of chaos, not by ordering it; but by walking through and mending it's fractures, ultimately; in story-form. 

In other words, a story itself, exists as a collection of ideas, scenes, fragments and fractions of a whole; a story comes together when time weaves the threads of the fabric into one axis of continuity. Where the distance between fragment "a" and "b" are no longer fractured; but necessarily separated. 

The "lattice" is in essence the emergent structure; not an engineered masterplan; but the work of countless infinitesimal small steps towards order. 

This is where ethics becomes the eternal, necessary structure; if dominions can fortify and strengthen with time, then it explains why  radical forms of chaos, e.g., totalitarianism or parasites even computer bugs, emerge as consequence of this natural (law or order?)
