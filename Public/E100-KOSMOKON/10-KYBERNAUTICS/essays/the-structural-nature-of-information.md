# "The structural nature of information, revealed by writing"
This is one of the most beautiful discoveries in all of Kybernautics.

## Abstract
This essay began with a fascinating discovery: Between "a" and "b" we can either infer implicit assumptions, or concretize explicitly. Even moreso, we can choose to zoom out of "a" and "b" entirely and ask "What's going on here?" Or the opposite: zoom into "a" and "b" individually, without a goal of unifying.

This came to be known as ["spheres of continuity"]() the "Meta", the "Macro", the "Micro" and the "Iota."

### A brief introduction into "Spheres of Continuity"

How do I move from "a" to "b?" Those gaps of information between "a" and "b" are where the story exists, the author must decide carefully how to bridge the gaps, or discard them entirely. 

This is the topological structure of information as we process it.

In summary, a story explains the ontological process of things, the "meta" of the "macro.", While facts and information are merely "iota" scale objects, or "ionetes." The micro and macro scales arise when we construct these worlds from the iota and the meta. Both must work in unison and feedback symbiotically.

The power of writing is in connecting parts. A story is when those parts are co-authored by the reader; imagination. 

The power of thought is manifold; not only does writing purposefully limit the bandwidth of information; it will reify it's structure so that it is visible and traceable.
