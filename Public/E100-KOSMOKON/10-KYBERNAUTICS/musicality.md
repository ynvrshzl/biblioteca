# Musicality
The creative process, editorial, and lifecyle of a musician's ability to write music, is an efflorescent blossom of life for Kybernetic study.

Music reveals a plethora of phenomenological human complex navigation processes and tools

## Semantic satiation
In the navigational, orientation, creative process, semantic-satiation or "temporary-loss-of-signal" is perhaps the arch-nemesis; the antithesis of order for a continous system, a living being.

The very fact that repetitive sound eventually loses it's continuity and meaning, means that we must continously calibrate and "refresh" or "update" our internal model of the piece. This is fascinating, becuase it means we can carry a model of the song, and work through it in the space of imagination.

## Continuity in music
Two types of continuity in music: Local time, and Universal/Global timeline. Local time requires progressiev layering to avoid signal-loss, and global time requires continous timeline for re-orientation.
