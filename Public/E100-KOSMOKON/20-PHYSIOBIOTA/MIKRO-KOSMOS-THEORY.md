## A propositional cartography of the Mikrokosmos

We may define "what does it feel like to be me?" as psychometrically or even kybernautically or even metronomically-defineable

We may begin by mapping awakefulness: "How much sleep does one have on average, per night?" This concretizes further into "How much quality sleep does one have per night?" And lastly "How much does one dream? What is the vividness of dreams?"

This continuum aims to map the range between extremities at the wakefulness scale: "a" or "b", Chronically exhausted or Fully awake.
