Your metaphors are powerful.

But metaphor is not automatically explanation.

For example:

- thoughts are not literally plants,
    
- depression is not literally weeds,
    
- consciousness is not literally a universe.
    

The metaphors are structurally informative —  
not mechanistic proofs.

This distinction protects the framework from drifting into pseudoscience.

- comparison,
