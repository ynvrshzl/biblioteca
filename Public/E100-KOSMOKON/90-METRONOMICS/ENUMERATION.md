It is essentially impossible to directly calculuate life metrics, i.e., 'complexity' or 'significance.' However, enumeration works becuase numbers are symbols, and symbols are the basis invariatns of language:

From a scale of 1 to 5, 1 being the lowest, and 5 being the highest. Estimation is the subjective parameter, enumeration is the precision.

This requires a Numbering+Index system to name things, rather than convoluted name-typography. This way [Information-Topology](Public/E100-KOSMOKON/10-KYBERNAUTICS/Information-Topology.md) is stable, while form is dynamic.
