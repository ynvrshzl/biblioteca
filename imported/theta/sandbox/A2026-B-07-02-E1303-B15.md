# Theory of Learning: How do we learn?
A general study of human learning

I believe there are oscillatory signal systems, not merely "stages"

- **Functional Ontology:** We ask questions of utility or function e.g., "how do i do x?" rather than "what exists?"
- **Vocabulary:** this is the classical form of studying. We memorize things
- **Intuition vs. Ergonomics** this is not essentially active memorization, rather, building [Muscle Memory](https://Wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_memory)
