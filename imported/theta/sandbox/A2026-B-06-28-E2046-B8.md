I would like to research this phenomenon... perhaps the Greeks (especially Aristotle) already explored these ideas extensively.

The balance between work and leisure (not sure if that's the correct word...)

It is incredibly challenging, especially in today's "always on, city that never sleeps (so to speak)" age. Not only is constant hustle unhealthy; it is also unsustainable at the biological human level!

What makes this incredibly difficult, is that in today's age, most of human life now includes some form of device technology. We are almost unconscious of this fact; that the very physical ontological existence of a device, disrupts our natural cycles as human beings. Of course, this naturally sprouts the first major argument; we have responsibility as human beings, to maintain balance in our lives, and cannot simply blame physical phenomena (including technological gadgets) as *the only* source of disruption. Coffee is a physical device. Fire is a physical device. Device, not in the same sense as a phone, but in the original etymological sense; a device is a catalyst for action.
