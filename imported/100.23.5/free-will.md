I never deeply considered the implications of Free Will... but it may be the most important problem in all of Philosophy.

Here is my understanding, and development of Central Thesis. We must first begin with understanding why this issue is not an issue of Science, but of Philosohpy.


"Not an issue of Science"

Science can only understand, or disprove, what is Observable either via Sensoria or Instruments. The instruments themselves must relay information that makes sense to us; not just stable Information Theory, in the Shannon sense.

If "Free-will" Determinism is defined as "Chemical Reactions" then that is defensible, from an Isolated or Closed Thermodynamic System. However, if you know anything about us Human beings; nobody can survive Isoalted or Closed from the world.

Therefore, the issue of free-will cannot end at the observation of chemical; for Free Will has Affects way beyond the Chemical. Secondly; we have no idea if we have the right to end at the Chemical. The Quantum is a stereotypical field used to "hypothetically" illustrate nothing is iherently "free." 

This however, is non-verfiable; non-falsifiable. Thereofre; Non-Scientific.

We baerly understand the brain, we're going to understand reality through Equations on Chalk? That is Absurd. Any Scientist who truly cares about and works towards the betterment of others, must understand how Absurd this problem is; It is not Scientific, it is Philosohpical.
