# Physiobiota I

## Historical Introduction

Much of the Human Experience, is deeply internal. This however, is misleading; much of our experience exists at the Transient border between the world outside, and the world inside. [^1]

Physiobiota: It's original conception, when I was in a fancy Grocery store, the dim orange lighting, the sweet, savory and salty aromas in the air, the warm temperature

It began by realizing "everyone sort of lives in their own bubble universe..." this later connected with a fundamental issue: "how can we understand each other? ourselves? what does it mean that my  life is differnet from yours? Why can't you seem to understand how I feel?" THis later becomes unified into one Theory: "We are experiencing life, in some ways, similar, in others, entirely different, in such ways that we may not even know could be possible." This is the most fundamental, and difficult of the Physiobiota Theories, becuase it implies an Ontological issue to Phenomenology; Life as we experience it, is one problem, but we suggest the true underlying issues of the Human Condition, is precisely in our Condition; our states, Physiology, Neurology... 

But this risks a Materialists bias; implying we Human beings are merely the Physical. Yet there is so much physical, we cannot observe with our senses. In some fundamental sense, this is why Mathematics, Science and even Stories, are so powerful; they all unify interfaces for abstraction. Of slices of Reality, we cannot directly Observe. Instruments, of the Episteme.

Given this, we have to conceptualize the Human condition in some sense.

## Physiobiota II

Concept of Terrarium, Biomes, Ecosystems, Biota and Worlds

The primary concept is "Biomes or Physiological Access"

Our feelings, emotions, sensations, and even our very memories, are dependent on our Physiological states. 

Certain memories are not accessible outside of the Physiological state in which they were encoded or consilidated or experienced. Certain emotions, feelings and even ideas, lose their intensity, outside of their Physiological state.

This implies our Physiology (and Mind-body connection) is no longer Pseudo-science; but an evident Phenomenology, explaining much of the mysteries of issues we have yet to be aware of - let alone - solve.

### Real-world implications
Knowing that we experience Life uniquely, is one thing, but applying it to real Science and Phenomenology; that is the Terminus Telos of this Field.
