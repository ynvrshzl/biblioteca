# Cyberoptics Representations and Format Theory

## 1.0 - Introduction


Design, Style and Aesthetic, are merely one such Dimension of Representation. The underlying problem, is much more profound: How do we Rearrange and Project Information such that both, the Human Being can process it according to our Sensory Capabilities, and construct defensible, effective Systems that will stand the test of time?

Every format has with it, Dimensions of Bias. A format, whether an Artstyle, A Syntax, or even Style itself, is not merely Aesthetic Facade; but Differential Dimensions of Expression. Every Format has a Necessary Bias; a Weight.

## 2.0 - Problem

No style is Arbritrary. No color is without relationship. No relationship without effect. When facing multiple design decisions, one may assume "This is a trivial issue, but why am I paralyzed?"

However, as with all things, we cannot merely push to one extreme: "Control all known boundaries of the System, to reduce representation costs." It is Epistemically impossible. We must work within simplified models and assumptions, becuase the Human Being is Built-for and Dependent upon Continuity &mdash; not Completion.
