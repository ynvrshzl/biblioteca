"To confront the suffering in one's life, diligently and voluntarity, is to live properly."

Those who do not confront some kind of problem in the world, are not working towards it's betterment. However; rather than adopting a Tyrannical point-of-view; I propose this is a personal contract with a meaningful existence itself; If you truly want to live a life that justifies the natural catastrophes, you must be willing to confront it

Those of us that live lives without pain, suffering or the harsh realities; are not lucky; but shielded from the brutalities of life. If we examine organic nature; it is not only beautiful; but extremely violent. So where do Ethics come from? Is it a Man-made construct? Or is there a deeper Symbiosis within all living things; an imprint of a design?
