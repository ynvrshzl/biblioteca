# Rheotics
Rheotics is the study of optimal Human cognitive performance; as guided by an ultimate Telos of Ethics. Not merely "Flow-based productivity" but "Fluidity."

The term originates from the Greek word "Rheo" as in "To flow."

One of the most underdeveloped areas of "flow-state" research, is the actual dynamics, symptoms, mechanics and lifecycle of flow-like cognitive-or-autonomous physiological states. 

Why does this matter? Our ability to engage in these cycles inherit to us, not only improves our working conditions; but also this state has been shown to reduce physiological suffering.
