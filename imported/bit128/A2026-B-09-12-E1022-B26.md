Yesterday I learned; it is okay to waste days. To waste time. Even to waste opportunities; for there is something far greater at work within the soul whom courageously charges forth towards good, despite having no instruction or knowledge on the path ahead.

We do not know how to do things properly without making mistakes. We do not know hwat is important without learning first what is not. And we only know what is good, by what is not. 

Have Grace for yourself; those with high-potential. Depression is only ever a few steps aside the narrow path; do not fall prey. That is what the Lord has revealed to me; Your Grace is Glorious; the only thing keeping me alive.
