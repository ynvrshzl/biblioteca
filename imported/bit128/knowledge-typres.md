Knowledge Types (k-types)

In Kybiotics/Kybernautics, Information is nonfinite and Essential; it's essence or substance is non structured to our scale; non-useable. 

If knowledge is nonfinite (Information), and Human knowledge is finite (Epistemology), then there must be stages between the nonfinite-ness of the Objective, Material world (...) and what we may understand (Episteme)

In other words, there is a Continuum of Knowledge at the Combinatorial scale (Potentiality): This Continuum stretches from Potentiality to Actuatlity. This becomes an integral object, as we discuss later; only a finite slice of the Continuum is ever useful.

Real-world Emergence

Building a little personal "Wikipedia" which eventually became the public wiki project [^1] exhibited this Phenomenon; even without Deliberate-Engineering;Knowledge is not made equal. There are Classes, Types and Categories of Data and Information; Knowledge is not neutral. The Human Nervous-System does not process even it's own memories equally; things are prioritized. [^2]

Classifications 

Two classifications of Knowledge are "a" and "b" Knowledge that will be integrated and abstracted upon, is type 'a', while knowledge that is designed for real-world action is of type 'b'.
