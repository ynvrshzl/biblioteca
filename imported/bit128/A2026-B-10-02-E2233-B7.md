One of the hardest problems in Lawrence's life, was not the problem of Good and Evil... it was the problem of Cruelty and Unfairness... "Nowever within this Universe, that my mortal eyes may peer... can I see the Logic behind cruelty, Lord. Why must it be so?"

"Why do some people live such lavish, happy, carefree lives, while others must suffer such brutatlity? How is one to avoid the posion of the Cynics, if there appears no other answer?"
