Calculating priority.

A landmark of Principia Economica is learning to manage time and energy and resources effectively. Priority appears as a crucial measurement to qualify and classify work especially when resources are finite or abnormally fragile.


Decompositors of Priority: 
- Relevance: Measures the general context of an item
- Invest: Measures the ratio at which resources are invested by an input function, and an output condition. The ratio displays the quantified weight
