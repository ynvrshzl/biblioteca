Phenomenologically (i.e., self-reported) how do Materialists, Agnostic or Athiests cope with the unexplainable, unjustifiable tragedies of life? Such as... sudden terminal illness of self or loved one, death of close loves ones (this is one of the hardest), and seemingly natural catastrophes? If there is no "Diety" to blame for or argument against; is the Athiestic worldview ultimately one of Futility? That "some people get lucky, and others don't, and it's just deterministic randonmess."

That may be where Nihilism stems from; a void of the existential and metaphysical. When the worldview no longer considers the potentiality of the spiritual or unknown; the known and material are all that remain; the sensory.

Why is this so dangerous? Isn't "grounding oneself int he moment" the basis of "mindfulness"?

I propose our beliefs are not only models of how we filter the world (worldview-theory); beliefs are the very contracts of trust. Contracts of truth when truth is incomplete, incalculable and intangible. In the case of systems; contracts of truth are essentially abstractions; we trust the abstractions are true to their Facade; this is especially true in the age of modern interface-driven computation. What happens when those Facade's reveal the underlying clockwork beneath? That's what happens when "perception is shattered like an exoskeleton, by a force it has not encountered before." This force, does not have to be Trauma-based, as the opening statement. Perception can actually be transformed and healed; we see this at every level of Physiology from Personality to Neuroplasiticity; experiences and sensoria are as real as transformation and after-effects.

The problem with the Materialistic worldview is that it halts at the physical world; even moreso; extremely dangerous ideas such as "simulation theory" or "sollipsism" state themselves as "non-falsifiable axioms of reality."

The problem with this kind of propositional theory is that an unstable mind can absolutely fall apart under the pressures of these information hazards. Several people report having nightmares about "Roko's basilisk" which began as a jest on a forum. The same can be said about Catholic Purgatory or Sheol (hell); ideas and imagination and perception exist at the borderlands between experience of the world (Sensoria) and theory of imagination and mind (Sophia, citing Aristotle.)

The reason I began this study with "what do people who believe in xyz do when abc happens?" is becuase it's one of the most powerful formulated "blueprints of phenomenology; how beliefs shape our perception and action."

Ideas are powerful. Beliefs are something like "Models of the world." Mathematics reveals the hidden order of the Physical world through extreme precision and control of quantity, change and measurement; studying life from bottom-up. Building from tiny axioms (self-evident truths), upwards to further abstractions and assumptions. At each stage of the evolution; Mathematicians are implicitly required to trust the underlying Abstractions. Without this trust, the structure explodes in complexity and our sensory systems; Overloaded and Incapacited for the real-world. Science studies life from Top-down; observation, hypothesis, research, test, refinement, etc. It's a cybernetic-lifecycle of regulation and feedback; not merely of control, as in the case of Mathematics.

Why does this matter? Twofold; Mathematics and Science exist at opposite ends of the Continuum; where one studies the world bottom-up, the other studies it from the opposite end. Eventually they find common ground and synergize; Physics, Engineering, etc. Those are the bridges of modern civilization.

Speaking of bridges; the second part is belief. Even as precise as Mathematics may be; Axioms themselves require trust that there is nothing beneath the granularity; and that our assumptions are built upon stable foundations.

So two parts; Structure and Trust. In some sense, Trust is Structural Integrity; we trust our assumptions, calculations and designs will stand the battle of Time and the forces of Nature.

I also propose the problematic aspect of Sophia; or the realm of Imagination and the Theoretical. When these gradients cross into our worldviews; Catastrophe ensues. No-one can build their own values; Nietzche paid the price for this (apparently he went mad, and his work had something to do with it.)

When Science and the Logical Human intellect, overstep it's boundary; we pay the price. Anything that presents itself as an ultimate truth must be investigated rigorously; just as a Scientist or Mathmetician. Nothing is self-complete or self-evident. Math requires language; Language requires Human beings; Human beings require sources of Life; naturally, Life itself must require something outside of it; Nothing is self sustaining; even the observable Life contains micro, macro and iota scales.

Infact, this is where I propose something like "Phenomenological divisions of Spacetime"; including the "Meta, Macro, Micro and Iota." Alongside a proposition of the very Materialistic world we are examining: "Essence, Function, Form, Affect." However, we should reserve these works for a later discussion

So rather than debating Metaphysics i.e., "Does God exist?"... I propose a radical prospect; Phenomenology, Science, Religion and Mythology must co-exist in the integrated, Ethical Human life. Failure to integrate these Fundamental disciplines may lead to a world with more suffering; based on the bloodbath of History; even if not all people are aware of it.

Religious institutions are often mistrusted for their Dogma; while Scientific Enterprises often descend into Tyranny when the Intellect rules over the Ethical. Science cannot define Ethics becuase the very Axiom of Science exists outside of itself; "That the world is made up of observable order, that the order is intelligible, that the laws governing the Universe can help us construct with it and solve problems, That the truth is worth pursuing, that Society benefits from Science, and that the understanding of this world is a noble pursuit." None of that is remotely Scientific; it is Proto-physics; Existing somewhere in the Existential and Metaphysical.

Science should study the order of the Tangible, Natural world, Religion should carry where it cannot explain, and Mythology should make the Religious, tangible. These almost form somewhat of a "Unity"; where one ends; the other begins. That is a necessary separation of concerns; it's one of the most basic systems principles; separate things by responsibility so that the system does not collapse under scale. And God knows Human life can scale into extremes of Disorder and Complexity; yet our Physiology is finely tuned (Neuroplasticity, Belief, Intellect, Logic, Creativity, Problem-solving, Ethics, etc.) such that we can withstand much more than the apparent Materialist worldview.

This is probably the "Judge's gavel" for this argument; what do you do when you can no longer process sensory data/information properly? Such as in Anhedonia or Fatigue; what does one do? This is precisely where we walk not by sight (or sound, or touch or sense) but by faith.

As a meta-argument, this is quite structure; but I explicitly do not care for it's intellectual bullet-proofness; I want to make sure these kinds of ideas are rigorously sculpted such that in the case of a real human being; they would change their worldview for the better, just as Jordan Peterson's work did for me.

My final argument is simply; whether we believe in Catholicism, or Bhuddism, or "Nothing"; our actions sow the seed of our Autonomy and Decision-making; and that shapes the world we live in. What's fascinating is how this Terminus eventually leads back to the beginning; the "Alpha" argument; "What is a Human being to do, given how infinitesimally small a single lifetime is when compared to the vastness of the Universe? What defines a Human life as significant?"
