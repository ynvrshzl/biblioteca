The Civics, specifically, the Logocrats/Logisticians believed in the power of the word; the "Logos." They believed that all problems could be solved within the Youniverse; and the the word was the medium. 

They believed that consciousness did not need to be preserved in the wicked technologies by the teologicians or the passive hedonism of the (p[ethos]); but that the word itself carried life just as blood itself carries life.
