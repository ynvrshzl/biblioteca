Time makes all things orderly. 

Perceptually, time is extremely simple; there are only two directions in which time can exist; Forward (Future) and Backward (Past). Each unfolding event in time is known as an "Event" or the Current, the Present. Master Oogway said the "Current moment is a gift, that is why it is called the present."

Managing our time, energy and decisions is a forward-looking path. Integrating and learning from our memories is a backward-facing path. Both a necessary for the current moment. 

These are the finite resources.
