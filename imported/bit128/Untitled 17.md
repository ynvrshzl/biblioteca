The intangibility of subjects, but made tangible by human professions.

A software program is not "just a window with some logic"; it is the result of thousands of invisible Human communication efforts. The abstraction of the work is so intangible; we never get to see how much really goes into building things that stand the test of time.

This is why professionals are the only source for ethics; the professional embodies their profession.
