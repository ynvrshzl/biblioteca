I believe the Philosophical, Greek terminology for "caring" would be "Enthusiasm," which is far more Etymologically salient. The word "Enthusiasm" remotely decomposes into two significant parts: "En" meaning, "Within" and "Theos," meaning "God." The word essentially translates to an abstract idea; "To be imbued with the spirit in Life, of Good and the Divine Order."

Philosophically, Theologically; even Phenomenologically; to be Enthusiastic is perhaps the highest form of "Caring" we know of. The reason this is a central emphasis of study, is becuase I realized many of the most devastating Psychological illnesses and Human states of living; may be abstracted as "a general carelessness or lack of immersion with a reason worth existing for." In other words; lack of enthusiasm isn't merely a lack of "positive emotion"; it is a lack of any drive whatsoever. Almost a "dopaminergic limbo state"; as examined extensively with Laboratory rat experiments. In which Scientists feed rats excessive amounts of Dopamine, within a structure enviornment; and eventually the rat loses to will to live in said enviornment; even when source of life (food) is available and visible.

That is perhaps the hallmark of the Human-condition; it is not Philosopical pondering; it is a fundamental truth of our nature; observable from the most Empirical of Biologies to the most Mythological of imaginations.
