Theory of Continuity

What differentiates abandoned seedlings from the sprouts of a tree? Is the idea, the seed, and is the tree in the seed? What is the process of the development of growth in Nature? Is it in the soil? In the life source? Does a tree grow strong by the water or the air? Is it in the enviornment? Is it an internal will to grow and endure? What differentiates an "ethos" from an "ethic?" It may be the Integrated enviornment; both the external and the internal, the intrinsic and the extrinsic.

At it's heart, this theory is studying a simple idea with deep implications; "How does one's reason translate into actual being?"
