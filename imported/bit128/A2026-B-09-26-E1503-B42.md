Title: "Theory of Relevance and Relata"

INTRODUCTION

Let's explore Relevance; especially as a primary function in neurology and cognitive orientation throughout spaces (information-space, decision-space, action-space, physical-space, etc.)

RESEARCH

How does relevance, as a function, play a role in the larger orchestra of everday coordination and navigation across spaces?

SAMPLE

Let's illustrate this in the real-world, using example of a scene; a surgeon's room. The room establishes context; performing surgery. The objects, machines and appartus themselves do not afford relevance; they primarily afford function (or use.) We see a tool and infer it's use, rather than it's Relevance may primarily be computed from "context", "necessity" or "circumstance."

OBSERVATION

The object, tool, assistant or general system affords function and use, while time and situation establishes relevance. This means that tools, technologies and systems must serve a purpose outside of function. (...this is orthogonal to Aristotle's Potentiality vs. Actuality...)

VARIABLES

In other words, a surgeon's room provides a window into how general systems work harmoniously as a "lifecycle." Coordiantion of nonfinite systems (tools, machines, people, time, decisions, etc.), and how those systems interact symphonically, from the macro to the iota (surgeon's fine motor motions) all depend on relevance to orient the future.
