Section B: "Information Ergonomics"

SE and Mathematics are deeply complex fields, often, interlinked with one another; what is true in one, may be deriven in another, and vice versa. As the foundation of Computation, Math is essentially composed of, and understood largely by Hierarchies of Abstraction. This is single handedly the most significant structural invariant at the bedrock of mechanical mathematics, computation and information; because Hierarchies depend on Abstraction of relata.

Once the system becomes sophisticated enough, parallel processing and advanced computation allows high-dimensional circuitry to emerge... what was once limited to fine constraint, had now become something more complicated than any single person could understand. By design, that may be necessary; the Computer eventually became a petri-dish, modelled after the Human brain that created it.

What's fascinating here, apart from the incredible ingenuity of proper Human work... is the problem of detail processing... I think it is the heaviest problem...

Section C

Illustrating this further, a Mathmetician would study basic arithmetic, then work their way up the chain of rules, relata and patterns (as we have recently studied...)

I hypothesize, by the time the Mathmetician has had sufficient time to learn a concept, the SE has accumulated a swath of information. There is a problem however; the Human being has fine-tuned Cognitive-constraints. The Human being is not a processing machine; the Brain has a very specific architecture; and learning how to work with it, arguably, is no longer a choice today.

We are pressed with information everyday, and we do not know how it is affecting us at large. I believe, the answer is to reduce the pace of information processing, create distance wherever possible, and maintain Continuity. Those are somewhat already actionable schemas.
