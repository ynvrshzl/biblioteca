Concept of "Language, Systems, Machina and Strata"

Let's envision code as instructions, not systems. The systems emerge as machines begin communicating and integegrating into system-like behavior; emergence. This is the cross between entropy and synergy.

For example, building a graphical component class is one system, or plane of strata. 

By realizing the workload and reponsibility, we can avoid stressing the sensitive cognitive faculties.
