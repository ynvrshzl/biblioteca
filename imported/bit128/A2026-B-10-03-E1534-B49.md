I am working on a very odd, loose framework for "meaning." From the bottommost, axiomatic to the most powerful of Phenomenological reality.

The framework begins with an observation; if Semantic-satiation is true for visual-and-auditory stimuli... is it revealing a more significant role in our perceptual-processing systems? The bio-machinery, beyond the material? (deeply metaphysical, pause necessary.)

If everything is "incomplete" and requiring of context, in order to be made sense of and processed for it's meaning... what if the context is the oppposite of a thing? What if we know a thing, by it's opposite or it's relata?

However we must acknowledge the risk; the world is not merely made of the material, visible or underlying processes. It cannot merely be; we only konw of the material by the instruments of Science and the Ideas of Philosophy and the Theory of the Intellect. Powerful as they may be; they can be destructive; just as objects without proper gravitation and orientation to a good; an Ethos + Logos.
