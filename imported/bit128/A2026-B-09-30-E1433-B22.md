Some of the fascinating properties of networks, especially knowledge networks, is captured as "Models of externalized Human memory." The Architecture, emerges when patterns are stable; and writing often reveals several patterns, naturally inherted from Lingusitics, but also the Phenomenological patterns; topic clusters, re-emerging ideas, re-orbiting.

Infact, the concept of orbiting, is a central thesis in the "Mikrokosmos theory." Thoughts orbit around consciousness and metaphorical celestial matter. Thoughts, memories, knowledge, ideas... these metaphorical objects have a weight... a significance.

One of the most fascinating properties is "Continuity Ressurgence", in other words, the reason we externalize ideas, is not merely to externalize noise; but to capture their form, in their fundamental purpose. Such that they may be re-discovered at a later time. We may envision this almost as "The Forge in which formless ideas are given form." In other words, this is where "naming" is an integral aspect, but the Lingusitics axis is one of many.
