Environment: Underground superhighway of Chicago 2035. Lighting: Artificial pure lumen 9k lux bright light. Sounds: Futuristic, Mechanical whirring of Detective Spooner's automobile flurrying at superspeed.

The Scene begins in the underground superhighway of Chicago 2035. Brightly lit with artificial light, Detective Spooner's futuristic automobile (Audi RSQ) eludes an alien spaceship. Silver-toned and reflective, the car captures a flurry of lights, barrelling by, capturing the dangerous speeds of the superhighway. The sounds of Spooner's vehicle, sonically similar to torpedoes blazing by.

As the camera emphasizes towards the windshield of the automobile, Detective Spooner (daddy) is seen relaxing in cruise-control. Laid back in his seat. Eyes closed. 

Slowly, Detective Spooner opens his sleepy eyes. His expression of confusion and "seriously?" showcases his attitude. As the industrial-grade truck, seemingly defies the laws of traffic, slowly positioning itself horizontally. Blocking Spooner's automobile from escape. There appears to be another truck, just behind him. He is locked. Detective Spooner, cynically says to himself: "There is no way my luck is that bad."
