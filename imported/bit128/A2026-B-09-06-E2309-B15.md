"Thread-Theory"

A proposition framework, unifying the manifold abilities of the Human-intellect, including but not limited to: Creativity, Problem-Solving, Learning, Analyzing and Memory.

The proposed shapes of attention, include "Forthographic-Stringing", "Abstract-Threading", "Refactoring".

This theory uses a metaphorical-model of "Threads, Strings and Fabrics" as a stable tangent for identifying, studying and implementing non-tangible, a-structured Human-Intellectual abilities.

The purpose of this theory-or-field, is to study how human attention works across diverse contexts; pairing beautifully with integrated-ergonomics
