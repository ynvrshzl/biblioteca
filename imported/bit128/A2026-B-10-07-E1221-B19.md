Why is Sedation so dangerous?

Recently, I have been examining the problem in modern life... I find myself at the parting between sedation and meaning. We were definitely designed to feel and fight for something worth dying for; that is perhaps the source(?)...

However, there are also extremes; one cannot devour the world; a voracious appetite for meaning is often too heavy to shoulder. 

We see an extreme: 

"Failure to carry your Human-Reponsibilities, will fracture the very foundations which enabled the misuse of your liberty." -- Lawrence 'Law'

So the conscience has a specific design; neither too much nor too little; the "Goldilocks" perimeter.

This opens up the problem "how may I avoid wasting an entire life?" Which opens up the chaos storms which led to many of the subjects, explored in this project. Most ... "Etheosophia, the study of ethics and ethos and significance."

Returning to the original problem; why is sedation so dangerous?
