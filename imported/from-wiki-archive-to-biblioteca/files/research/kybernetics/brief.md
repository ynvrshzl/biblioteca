## Phenomenological breakthrough
I realized; we cannot explain life as merely mechanical determinisitci phenomena, not becuase we lack the sufficient understanding of the laws of the universe, but rather, becuase an explanation of everything leaves nothing to be formed. In other words; explaning everything is about as useless as explaining nothing; by the very definition.

This means we cannot merely explain life as "deterministic logic," even if we posessed the computational power to express such abstractions; they would still be our interpretation of a larger whole we epistemelogically (and necesarrily) will never comprehend.

I realized then; understanding is a form of control. Knowledge, is a form of control. However, it is only when we face the real chaos behind the interface; behind the knowledge; that we gain something even more valuable; ability, reiforced by experiential knowledge.

This is what separates an idea from a fact; an idea was processed by another living being, before it was externalized. A fact, remained... (okay this is too analytical.)
