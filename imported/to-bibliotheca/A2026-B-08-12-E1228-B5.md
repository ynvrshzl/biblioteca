Professor Theodore, inspired by John Lennox, a Scientist who believes with all his heart, in God.

Theodore has the nature of a gentle giant, but his intellect is needle-sharp.

"What I would give to see the world through your eyes... that brilliant mind of yours..." Theodore enjoys himself a good chuckle. "The abscence of shadow is by the prescence of light, but the abscence of light is not the prescence of the shadow."
