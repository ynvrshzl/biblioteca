# Symbiotics
Symbiotics is a field of study which aims to explain how we as human beings can ethically, symbiotically integrate our internal world, with the external world.

## Synopsis
When we want to understand how a part of information fits in the natural world, or is useful, we are essentially forming a symbiosis between the information and actuality; symbiotic implementation.

Sym. Symbiotics attempts to unify these distant worlds by providing real-world examples, hence "Sym." as a shorthand abbreviation for a "Real-world symbiosis example" not merely an implementation, but an integrated implementation.

A symbiotic integrates the input and the output, such that the processual intermediary, forms a ... where both ends are...

Examples of symbiotics in the real-world includes:
- __"Book":__ a symbiotic of medium, integrating vast quantities of material within a cohesive timeline. The book is as much a work of the author, as a resource for the reader.
- __"Music":__ Music integrates a unique symbiosis between the listener and the musician; moving to the same rhythm at both ends.
