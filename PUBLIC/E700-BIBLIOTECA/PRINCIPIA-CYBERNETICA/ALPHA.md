# Title
PHENOMENOLOGY AND COMPUTATION: A UNIFYING STUDY.



## Abstract

As soon as I sit down in front of the computer, my thoughts quiet, and I'm no longer able to imagine or think through problems. That's a problem; becuase not only is natural thought one of the most integral forms of psychological defragmentation (along with spoken, verbal language), but thought is generally, one of the most effective methods we have as human beings for problem-solving; one of the ways in which we work through difficult problems that would become noisy if externalized. And that's really a fascinating inquiry for a separate discussion. 


## As we may think
Essentially what we are doing when we are thinking is we are traversing something like an inner microcosmos. And a computer is another microcosmos.

It's almost as if the computer is by design and/or has become associated with throughput; output, productivity, work, focus, attention. So the argument is this; what if the machine itself, is most effective when it bridges the gaps between input, process and output?

- Input: human imagination, ideas, problems, phenomena, information.
- Process: transformation of input; computation and calculation itself; most of the computer exists here.
- Output: product, solidified material or improved understanding.
