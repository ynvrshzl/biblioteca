# What truly changes a person? What is a human being? What defines a person?
As I and You, and as we.

There is nothing more transformative, than the identity of a human being. To adopt responsibility is one thing; to be responsible. But to be a reliable character, means responsibility has become interwoven with your every decision. 

What transformed my life, and continues to, is the image of the mother; "Maternia." A mother is a warrior figure, no less in might than the father. The mother, however, carries the burden of life within love; that to love you must accept the responsibilities embedded.
