# Template theory
The primal predator, arch-enemy in Kybernautics, is Chaos. Information overwhelming the sensite, finite human resources.

Templates and formats, establish 3 vital components for sustainability: Consistency, Pattern, Reliability

## Abstract
The study of template theory focuses on propositions of automation and redundancy abstraction, as generalized tools for information complex management.

Formatting, template, presets, and forms of automating redundancy, are key for managing complex information...

Before standards, stable structures must emerge. Patterns are authorable via chunking, meaning: `"101011010111"` becomes `"1010 1101 0111."` Ontologically, it is the same string of information, but phenomenologically, it reads as an entirely different structure. This is the basics of chunking.

## How do we author patterns?
