# Scale 
We recently had a discussion on my hypothetical model of complexity management and navigation: something like "a hierarchial-graph model" where the system is neither entirely hierarchial, nor completely interconnected like a network...

basically, the main idea that applies to real life is; scale. The micro and the macro. This has got to be one of the most fundamental aspects of wellbeing. Our ability to switch perspectives, to describe things in either granular detail, or lossy images... is absolutely fascinating... it's where the term "the big picture" comes from... but also "break it down" suggesting a large macro scale is where we orient ourselves, but action comes from sculpting... "breaking"... which has it's philosophical depth, for another discussion!

In a more serious sense; scale is our ability to tell the difference between;

- this thing appears huge becuase it is is occupying my periphery, a distortion of scale relative to perspective

- this thing is truly massive, regardless of my scale.

That is a mechanical definition of dynamics at first-person-scale. It's absolutely significant to understanding phenomenology; how we actually experience these mechanical dynamics, from lived synthesis.

Here is where it gets crazy; i hypothesize that this is the main mechanic, for specific states of human intellect; e.g., rumination, anxiety, depression, burnout, and even chronic fatigue...

...These are conditions that essentially construct an internal echo-chamber. It may stem from various sources, but *most* people who suffer with these issues, report "internal dialogue" which is more of an "internal monologue" since a dialogue is two-way communication. For example, rumination is a progressively narrowing downards spiral into fixating and focusing on negative aspects of one's experience. This is a phenomenon of scale; that which appears closer when we lose ability of scale.

This is exactly why mirrors on vehicles always print "objects in mirror are closer than they appear" it is a problem of scale distortion, based on relative position. If we were examining the physical space from a third-person perspective, ie.., the "macro" or "big picture, we would accurately distinguish between subject and object; the distance between things, relative to their true scale.

This is almost impossible from the first-person perspective. I think it's why some individuals suffer so tremendously inbetween their ears (so to speak)... with storms of madness and pain that is indescribably; even if it is very much a physical process.

That is where mythic and art makes the invisible, visible. And that has got to be one of the most important forms of remedy for the human condition. This study focuses on the mechanical physics of this phenomenon, but in all honesty, these topics are most powerful when applied to the mythic scale; becuase that's the fourth wall that the perspective engine of human consciousness is missing!!!

This appears to be a psychological function, that follows the laws of physics, but in an intangible space (the intrapersonal.)

conditions which mimic (or cause) severe psychological disorders, may originate from a problem of scale (amongst other complex phenomena).
