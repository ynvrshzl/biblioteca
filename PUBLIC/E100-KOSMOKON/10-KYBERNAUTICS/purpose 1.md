# What is the purpose of writing?
Writing and language, is a powerful combination, arguably second-to-none, in terms of human phenoenological expression. However, writing has a very specific set of purposes, strengths and weaknesses. If one is to believe "writing will solve all my problems" or "writing is always the answer" or "i can do anything within my writing" we pay a price for over-credentializing the externalized power of language.

## "Format" theory
Other forms of externalizing exist for this reason, atleast, systematically speaking; language, specifically; written language posesses an intangible capacity for externalizing the intellect and information; this comes at a great cost.

Each format provides a tangible superface, and must subsume the rest; examples include
- __"Dancing":__ a form of rhythmic explosive forces and motions, integrating the body in relation to sound, rhythm or ...
- __"Storytelling":__ oratory, written or embodied
- **"Writing":** a form of externalized recorded medium...
