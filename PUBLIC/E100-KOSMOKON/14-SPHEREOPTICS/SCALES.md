Both the micro and the macro are necessary, and the iota a fraction of the micro. They are only sustainable insofar as they are integrated in gyroscopic-unision; meaning in continuity. In continous ... in continuity.

The micro and iota scales possess an extraordinary precision of detail and consistency. This is where a pianist practices a few scales, or individual technique, or a ballerina working on their poise, or isolated... 

However, the iota scales pose a constant risk of semantic-satiation; loss of local meaning, and loss of global awareness. This returns with distance, but is often neglected as "working hard" or "pushing through it." Much to the detriment of the performer.

The macro and meta scales however, pose an opposite effect; a load of meaning too heavy to bear or make sense of.
