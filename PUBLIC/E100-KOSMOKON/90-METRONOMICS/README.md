# Symmetrics

The idea for this language derived from how we can measure time in two distinct ways: precision or proportional. This proves that if we do not require precision for time-keeping; there must be other forms of measurement through which we human beings navigate the world and keep track of things

## Difference between Mathematics & Symmetrics

Whereas mathematics describes calculation by transformation via logic and precision, Symmetrics studies how we measure naturally as human beings, using proportion and scale to measure real-world artifacts, or non-precise objects.

## Problem

The problem to solve: how do we effectively compute and calculate quantity and quality, when precise values are not accessible?

## Proposition: "Naturalis"
"Proportion over precision"

Instead of counting time by precise, e.g., "2:30 PM" or "55 Minutes until lunch", we calculate via natural hierarchical, proportional, relative ranges e.g., "Noon by half" or "About a full cycle."

## Our natural sense of time

Our ancestors measured time in chunks of around 15 minutes. This naturally slices the hour into four quarters. They did not know precise time; so how did this come to be?

Cellular life contains ingenious time-keeping dynamics, from purely cellular clocks to atomic resonance, these "events" have an order, and "chronos" is the distance between these events.

As soon as the sun-dial was developed; we started mapping precision onto continuity; values of change across time.
