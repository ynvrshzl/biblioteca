Annemarie is a young woman striving for excellence in the city of Lost Angelis. 

Author's note: my clothing style is largely derived from her attitude towards life; sharp, terrifyingly brave but tipping over the edge between psychotic order and. Her life is a constant dance on the blade's edge; but her graceful poise and love of her craft, give's her the necessary respite to face even the most disgusting of situations, with elegance and dignity.

In her life, she witnessed extreme levels of second-hand trauma. "I will never be the same. Some part will be completely broken for the things my eyes have seen... and yet... I believe there is good in life... I believe there is a reason for us being here... and my task is to make that reason as sharp as possible to as many of you whom are willing to wield it. Reason, is a blade. We either dance on blades on ice, or they..."
