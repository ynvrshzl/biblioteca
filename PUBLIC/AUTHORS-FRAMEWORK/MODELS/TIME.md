# Title
"Time as a powerful model of stories, both for authoring and reading"

## Context
As the storms of ideas occlude and accumulate, I witnessed as they appeared to simply continue to aggreagate; I was struggling to make sense of how the layers of story would fit together into one medium; characters, worlds, lore, scenes, and general ideas. 

How these elementary parts of literature would react or synthesize cohesively together. This is where the Time; specifically the 'Timeline' becomes an integral model in literature, as both a unifying force for the author, and an experiential frame for the reader; a [symbiotic-framework.](PUBLIC/SYMBIOTICS.md)
