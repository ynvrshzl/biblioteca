The "Civics" were a classified group of people throughout [Youniversal History.](domains/com.youniverse/Youniversal%20History.md)

## The Post-Empiricists
The Post-Empiricists were a group of mostly Doctors and Lawyers whom attributed "God in Knowledge." They however, constructed a "Brain of Babel" or "Neurobabel" after worshipping the Human Brain and intellectual capabilities.

They believed that by knowing all things, one would solve all problems. However, the more they began to study the world, the more they realized everything was interconnected, in ways that could never be statically analyzed. (Emergence).

This is where the "Emergents" diverged from the Post-Empiricists, developing their own Ideaology and Socieity, founded on the belief that "Our tools can solve all problems, including the fundamental problem of Emergence."

This however, will end up in a terrible fate. Most of the Society either goes insane (studying everything there is to know) or ...


This is where the first form of "Rebels" emerge; the Wuweicists
## The Categorists & Categoriticians
"They believed that all problems and all circumstances in life, could be solved by finding the correct categories... knowing where to look... unlocking the clockwork"

They laid the conceptual foundations to understanding what would eventually become [The Kosmokon](domains/com.youniverse/lore/kosmokon.md)

They shared many of the Traditions and Knowledge of the Post-Empiricists
