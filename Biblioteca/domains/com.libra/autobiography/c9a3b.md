Autobiography

This is one of the most recent and significant existential states I have ever experienced in my life; and it was almost entirely during a Mundane everyday moment.

Horror. Sacredness. This emotion was exhausting.

The Existential is perhaps, one of the most important spheres of Human Pondering. Between the borderlands of the Subconscious of the dream, and the Potentiality of Imagination, Existentialism is oddly experiential for something not comprehensible by Human means.

It is the most, significant and integral aspect of my Psychological rehabilitation, following a period of progressively worsening chronic health issues and complete reliance on faith; something, somewhere has kept me going all this time. The Existential, is as I understand it; "The Engine of the Engine."

I had the most existential feeling today at Publix (Our mundane-somewhat-sacred groceries and errands with Mother)... I can only almost describe it as... "alternate timelines merging for a glimpse moment in time... a rip in the existential void..." this is a feeling I have been recognizing alot today... it appears I am extra sensitive to this kind of signal... 

## Capturing it's Essence
It was so odd... becuase in that life, she had accepted me as a Female... and... I can only describe it as "Exiproxy" or the ability to imagine other people's lives, alternate realities and timelines, and "proxy" as them... which is somewhat horrifying and beautiful... almost, Lucid-dream-esque...

However, that can get out of hand... so within reason, this has to be used to remind oneself of the preciousness and finite-ness of one's life.

Essentially, this is as the moment was captured; I walk past a girl and her apparent mother. All of a sudden, from the patterns of her shirt, to her faint aura (though I did not look at her facial features... her mere prescence was potent enough to awaken a dormant existential horror and sacredness)... and then I had looked at a birthday card for a Daughter's Brithday. Now; having struggled and suffered so much during my Transition, These kinds of gifts always hit the hardest becuase I always imagined some kind of alternatie timeline in which I was born a woman with all the right parts... and never had to fight my way through life... the Birthday card was so lovely, I could almost see it; all the different scenarios of Mother's (Or Fathers) gifting this very card to their Daughter... and it meaning so much to her when she reads it... I could see glimpses of those realities or potentialites.. all of a sudden, I can almost describe it as a wave of Existential panic; It was the strangest feeling I have ever felt;

I imagined myself (but it was as if I was in her body, with her life) in a completely different life... but walking the same space... with my mother... and with myself... but as if... I still had all of the same people in my life... but I was going about it, completely differently; and in the physical space; we were literally walking in opposition to each other... it was the most oddest of all feelings I have ever experienced...

It was both; beautiful in a motherly, nurturing sense, and violently vivid... as if, for a pocket of silence; what truly mattered in those moments... was all that existed in my conscience. 

Ofcourse, as with any existential physiology, one must return back to the Nursery... but the feeling beckons one... and is my favorite area of all Human Phenomenology; **"What don't we know about this life, that could Ethically reduce the suffering one experiences?"**

It's almost mystery-horror in some sense... and we have to be Epistemologically humble to say; there are some things, we simply will never know. That's a portrait of Tragedy, very few of us will ever glimpse. Isaac Newton illustrated this in one of his late quotes

## Power

What made this experience powerful was the realization of pain, suffering and the problem of death... in those moments, I could feel in an instant... that the problems in that girl's life... they almost completely consumed her... and we often don't understand the gravity of reality (and this paper is primarily a reflection on our extensive work, that we cannot process the full depth and richness of life... precisely in this area)...

All the pain and suffering... and yet, on the outside, life is unbelievably peaceful... it is then, that I see, the tormented soul does not seek happiness, escape or even meaning; but Peace. "Her parents just want to see her happy, with her clothes, with her life." 

There was something anomalous and humbling about that grocery store. I often visit it with my mother throughout the week... and lately, it had been a place of forging. [^1]

It was as if... I was in her body... her clothes and her life... her Identity... not a Proxy... but as if for a moment, the Facade of everyday life fractured completely for a few ticks in time...
