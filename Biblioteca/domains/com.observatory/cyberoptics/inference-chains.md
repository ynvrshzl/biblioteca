## The Logic of Information Processing
There are a manifold of possible arrangements for systems information processing; yet only a few in nature, withstand the forces of information overload. This is precisely why, Neuroscience, Information Processing and Navigation are Orthogonal to the field of Cyberoptics; It Unifies our understanding of Bio-machine, Problem and Strategy.

Now we are getting at the inner workings of information processing, so much so, that Computer Scientists could not effectively model early Artificial-Intelligence without sampling from Neuroscience; in other words, building a computational brain to solve information problems.

The brain, in some sense, is a mass bio-automata for processing mass amounts of infomration. However, this information is fundamentally incomplete without Neurology; the Nervous System and our Sensory Data.
