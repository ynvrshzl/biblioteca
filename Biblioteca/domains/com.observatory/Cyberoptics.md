# Cyberoptics
"Cyberoptics" is a field studying how the Human Being navigates Systems and Information in the world, by using the Human Sensory "Optics" as a Metaphor to illustrate fundamental concepts for real-world application.

Cyberoptics itself is part of a larger series in "Understanding Human Abilities through Cyber-senses (Extended beyond the Five.)" is Adjacent-and-Integral to [Cyberneering](domains/com.observatory/Cyberneering.md)
