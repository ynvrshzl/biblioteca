###### SECTION 1.2 - INTERRUPTS
Interrupts are by far the most problematic errata I encounter on a daily basis. 

A metaphor to illustrate just how problematic Interrupts are: "Working with interrupts is arguably worse than not working. Would a surgeon perform delicate operations during an earthquake? Would an artist apply makeup in an uneasy ride?"

Now, in the context of an OS, this isn't a problem; quite the opposite. Interrupts are necessary to send signals to hardware, to work with sensitive memory, or even to break loops. That last function, breaking loops, is infact the very same phenomenological power of interrupts. Interrupts are external cues not scheduled or controlled.
