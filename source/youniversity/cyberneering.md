Principles of Systems Design and Construction (Cyberneering):


Principle 1: Structure

This Principle proposes: "Stable abstractions require structural sufficiency strata-below."

Information without meaning, is structure. Matter, mass, measurement. Meaning is emergent, consequential, effectual, not deterministic as the materialist would imply. In Systems design, Ethics is the Telos of all Systems; The "Terminus" a final arbiter. 
"It doesn' matter how good your ideas are, if the building collapses, it is the engineer's fault." [^1]
