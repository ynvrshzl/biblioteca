# Infonautics
My warning is that there is a fragile edge of our human intellect and psyche; they are not the same thing. Intellect is a byproduct of the state of psyche. A fragmented, errata-laden state of being, will manifest in exactly the same effects in intelligence.

Likewise, there are things we should not look at, explore, try or question; these are "volatile elements, information hazards." We have no idea how complex things may be, and we don't understand how to approach the many mysteries embedded in everyday life... so I propose a pledge of humility; **go no further you absolutely have to.**
