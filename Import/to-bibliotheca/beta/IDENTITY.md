# Identity & Purpose
I Hypothesize the strongest gravity for human purpose is identity.

If one asks "What is my purpose?" It is synonymous with "Who am I?"

A misconception about identity, is "This is who I am." However, Identity is far more about socioecological influence, than a self-centered definition, infact, the self is really only the horizon between these stratospheres of being. [^2]

Identity is only as defineable as it's embedded-ness in the larger context; i.e., Socioecologically.

Who I am, what I give, how people see me.
