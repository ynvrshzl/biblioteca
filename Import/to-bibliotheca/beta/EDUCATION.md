## Applicability & Implementability

Applicability & Implementability essentially bridge the distant gaps between the Function of a subject, the Form of a subject, and the Human will to process complex information, over sustained lengths of time.

This is significant in Education; and often under-discussed. The will to learn, is not only the openness to novel sensory data; arguably learning is truly the willingness to be incorrect, unprepared, or humbled by a master (in archetypal terms. (this proposes theories on personality types and the willingness to learn, however, that is adjacent and far outside the scope of this study.

))

Some context on how this Hypothesis was formed; After studying brief material on Category Theory, Trigonometry, and Physics, I realized something fascinating; without a Reason or Telos; the material is perceptually processed as "noise" by the nervous system. When an instructor is presenting information on presentation slides, or flipping through text books; it reads as a script, rather than an unfolding process. This is significant becuase the hypothesis diverges into two branches; Story and Telos.

Two of the most signficant, understudied models in Human flourishing and Psychology.

As soon as one understands the real-world purpose of these complex fields; Reason propagates downstream to even the most complex of ideas within the field. The story, is the processual model of evolution; why complexity emerges.

Essentially the Arch-enemy of Education, may not precisely be boredom or Economic underfunding (very real problems, nonetheless); It may be how Education is structured Phenomenologically; unjustified noise, with insufficient reason for expension resources.

Atleast, that is the hypothesis: "Reason is the map between Function, Form, and Application." The function of a subject is to explain "what" is known within a given sphere of human knowledge. The form of a subject is the interpretation of that function; i.e., the structure of a module, or the chalkboard of a professor. These are "forms" or "methods" of education of the underlying subject matter. The essence of the subject, however, is in it's appliacation or implementability in the real-world; outside of the subject itself.

This is fascinating becuase it requires us to "zoom outward" of the subject, into the "metasphere" so-to-speak, and ask the essential question "why does this matter?"

In the case of Category Theory, we may learn about deeply cohesive, organized ideas. No amount of elegant organization or sophisticated systems of education, can replace the inherit human drive for reason or purpose. That is the primary hypothesis.

However, becuase this is a study of human-educatory, this proposition has to be assessed with the actual human phenomenological conditions; From a Psychological analysis, there appears to be two distinct classes of reason: Intrinsic Reason, and Extrinsic reason. Intrinsic reason is most common among Mathmeticians and brilliant individuals, though the downside is they often lack the ability to translate this inherit motivation into the realworld. That real-world applicability, is the Extrinsic sphere of reason; how to synthesize knowledge into action or ability.

I think I'm converging on something like a Theory of Education... or how we human beings optimally; naturally learn, at a general scale.
